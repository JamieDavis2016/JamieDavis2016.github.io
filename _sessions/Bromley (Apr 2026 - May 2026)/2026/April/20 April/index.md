# Serve receive and enhancing our block
- Players - 8?

- Balls - 4?

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30

# Focus
Building around our serve receive and our block, what are we watching? what is the purpose of our block?

# Warmup 
- Playing a short distance of the 3m line
- 2 vs 2, no blockers, service in with a downball (no jump) attack, once feeling warm and ready, add an attack in

Score a point each time we can get an attack from the rally

## Part 2
- Adding in a blocker, every time the ball goes over, swap who blocks
- Adding a blocker and a defender behind, what are we seeing as a defender? What are we looking at as a blocker?

Score a point each time we get a touch on the ball as a blocker

# Activity 1 - Serving and serve receive
- Looking at building on last week, but now you can start anywhere and have to read the ball, 4 in the receive on one side
- Setter is going to start front court
- The serving side has 2 / 3 with 2 / 3 blockers, reading the attack and trying to transition out to it

## Adding attacking options
If we have enough players, going to have 5 on one side and 3 on the other, with the 5 having a middle, front court hitter and a back court right side hitter

# Activity 2 - Serve receive, is different to in rally play
- A wash drill,

- 4 vs 4 or 5 vs 3

Quickly going through the rules of behind and in front with your players

## Part 2 - Changing the free ball
With the free ball changing, it might be a cross hit, a line hit, or something else and we have to figure out how to play against it, the goal is to always have an attack back

# Game
If we block we get a point
If we transition based on where the attack is we get another 
And if we get an attack with a 2 step approach a 3rd point

Can score all 3 points in rally
