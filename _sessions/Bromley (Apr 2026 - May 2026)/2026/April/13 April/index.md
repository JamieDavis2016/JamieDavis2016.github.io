# Reading the serve and creating an attack
- Players - 7?

- Balls - 4?

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30

Our first session as a group, lets see how we want to play and where we want to play

# Focus
Reading the serve and running an attack, Creating a system for our games

# Warmup
2 vs 2 (half courts)

3 contacts

With a serve that's in

# Activity 2 - Serve and SR
3 Passers, 1 setter

Passers are going to start close to the 3m line

With 3 on the other side
Playing the service out

With the 3 side if you defend the ball into the court you gain a point

Attacking side, if you communicate a shot and the attacker plays it you get 2 points


# Activity 3 - Cross Court pepper (cooperative)
Aiming for 15 touches cooperatively to start

4 vs 4 (2, 4, 5 and 6) Open

Serve in, then a free ball if the play ends

- Starting with passes only to 15
- Then a standing attack / down ball to 15
- Then full attacks

# Activity 4 - 6 vs 2 or 4 vs 4
Change to find our positions if we do 6 vs 2

If it's 6 vs 2 we will make it so that the 2 side, if they touch the ball they win the point (any touch)
