# Movement and control

# Focus
Can we control the ball with our passing? Looking at if we can track and keep the ball alive for as long as possible

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half courts
- Can only dig or volley, must be 3 contacts
- Live serve or a throw, anywhere behind the 3m line

Have 4 minutes to score as many points as you can against your opponent

# Activity 2 - Serve receive variant
- 4 on one side
- 2 on the other

4 has 2 passers with bands slightly higher than ankles, we then have a setter and an attacker

other side has a server and a blocker

After 7 points, swap the places with the bands

# Activity 3 (if 2 is a struggle) - In pairs
Ball throws behind and you have to pass with your forearms or volley, with bands around your ankles

This is looking at our shuffle step and creating a solid platform


# Activity 4 - 4 vs 4 cross court pepper
- Positions 2, 4, 5 and 6
- Digs and volleys to start, can we get to 21 touches in a row before moving onto an attack?

With markers down for deeper parts of the court

Keeping the same shuffle when we get to the ball

# Game
If our serve receive is close to the player at 2, you score an extra point
