# Back to movement

# resources
- 24 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 / 2 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 2 courts

# Focus
Can we control the ball with our passing? Looking at if we can track and keep the ball alive for as long as possible

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 warmup games
- Catch and throw

- 1 contact
- 2 contacts
- 3 contacts

# Activity 2 - Band passing, In pairs
Ball throws behind and you have to pass with your forearms or volley, with bands around your ankles

This is looking at our shuffle step and creating a solid platform

- 1 side is the server and another player (2)
- other side is a 4 with 2 players having bands as receivers

The 4 side is aiming for a pass from the 2 players with bands, with a set and an attack

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4
- 2 front, 2 back courts

- 3 on the receive and then goes to 2 front and 2 back
- Rotate through when the ball drops

Ball thrown in from a pair waiting off, rotating around
