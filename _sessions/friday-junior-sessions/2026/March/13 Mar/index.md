# Adding volleys into our play
- Players - 24

- Balls - 6+

- 2 courts

- 1 hour 30

# Warmup
- Ball throws and attacks to warm up body

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 queen / king of the court
- Using our serving from last week, a service in and playing the 2 vs 2, if you miss serve rotate behind the other side

Playing 2 vs 2 trying for a pass, set, hit

If you win the point you stay on the receiving side, if you served and won rotate through to that side

Playing for 3 touches and over (if we can!)

# Introduction to Volleying
Quick demonstration with how we use our hands to volley and pushing with our legs

- In pairs Volleying over the net, higher than the antenna, using legs and hands

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3
- We're now going to have a setter and introduce the volley on the 2nd touch where we can!

The volley needs to go higher than the antenna, and can try an attack off of that

# Game 
- Trying to now use the volley in our game
