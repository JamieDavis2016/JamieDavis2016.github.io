# Creating an attacking opportunity

## Focus; Creating an attacking opportunity from serve receive

# Resources
- 11+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Over the net pepper, cooperative pepper
Getting into groups as a passer, setter and an attacker

The attacker is going to stand and hit the ball

The feed is a free ball and every 5 balls we'll rotate the attacker, setter and passer


# Warmup 2; Working on some attack approaches 
### Purpose: A quick look at our 3 step attack approach with a thrown ball

We are going to look at a 3 step approach, l-r-l with our locator step being on the 3m attack line and getting into a big last 2 steps

In pairs a ball thrown and an attack to start


# Activity 1: 3 vs 3 half courts

Start with a throw from the serving side

2 passers and 1 setter

The passer on the left becomes an attacker, so pass and attack

Working on that left - right - left if you're a right handed player

# Activity 2: Hitting lines

3 on one side at position 4 (in a queue), one of those becomes a passer and rotates, we rotate from passing to setting then setting to attacking, our setter is at position 2

Then there's a ball being fed in from the other side for a passer and a setter

We will replicate this on both sides working on creating that consistent footwork as well as ways to get to the ball for an attack
