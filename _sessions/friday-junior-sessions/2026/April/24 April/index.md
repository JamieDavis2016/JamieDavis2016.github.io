# Ball control to enable an attack

# resources
- 24+ players
- 10+ balls
- 2 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 2 courts

# Focus
Ball control to enable an attack. Looking at how we can get the ball always controlled and high into the air

# Warmup - Volleytennis 
2 vs 2 Volleytennis

- both sides have a ball and start with a throw, have to throw both balls at the same time, 1 touch and the ball goes over, then rotate players around

- Have to win both ball points to knock the other 2 players out

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 3, double touch volleyball
- Half court game, rotate every 3 points, player that's off rotates into the 2, 1 of the 2 moves to be the 3
- A serve from anywhere behind the 3m line
- The player that receives or defends the first ball, has to defend the first ball and then pass / set the 2nd ball to their partner (OR they can send the 2nd ball over)

- Start with sending the ball over with a volley or forearm pass, add the attack in later

# Activity 2 - Four corners
- 3's in 2 halves of the court, 1 player to take and then rotate round
- 1 side of the net passes straight, the other passes cross
- The side that passes straight, throws both balls in to start the drill.

Go for 2 minutes before we rotate

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4
- Starting with a throw over, dig to self, set to partner for partner to attack (same person that defends), OR they can send the ball over
- Rotating when the ball drops to start, once familiar we rotate when the ball goes over the net
- Positions 1, 4, 5 and 6 are on court, 2 and 3 are out of bounds

# Game
6 vs 6 
- Playing with 3 touches, if the pass is higher than the antenna they score an extra point
