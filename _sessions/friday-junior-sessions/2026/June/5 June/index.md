# Defence on serve receive

# resources
- 12 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 130 hours
- 1 court

# Warmup - Butterfly passing
- A server, 1 passer and a target

Every serve you rotate to be a passer, passer rotates to target

Looking at ball flight and getting into positioning, reading the ball and getting underneath

# 1.2 - 2 passer butterfly
- 2 passers, 1 setter and a target

- Serving still, rotating in and working on a 2 person pass, working with another passer

# Activity 2 - Bands (half court)
-- This is if the passing footwork is struggling and only a tool to help with movement

- a server, 2 passers and a target
- The 2 passers have resistance bands on their ankles and are working on getting to the ball with the bands

Rotating round every 7 balls

# Activity 3 - Server and Serve receive
- 3 servers, on both sides
- 3 passers, a setter and a target for the hitters (hitter catches)
- rotating from 5 as a passer -> 4 as a target -> 6 passer -> 2 setter -> 1 passer -> serving next

# Activity 4 - Playing the rally out
- Working on the transition from last week

Starting with a free ball over, getting the transition in, 6 vs 6

# 4.2 - Playing the rally out
- Now having a server and once that ball is finished, a free ball in to play

Bringing this session and the transition work from last week in
