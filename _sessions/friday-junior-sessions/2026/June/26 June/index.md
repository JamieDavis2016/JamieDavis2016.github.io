# Using your platform angles and generating setting force

# resources
- 9 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup;
-> brief introduction on how to teach the volley, using these cues, arms high, and push with legs (going through the motion of volleying in it ) 

-> in pairs, start close together and go further apart, trying to keep these cues in tact the further you get and coming back together 

# Activity 1; Playing 2 vs 2 
-> Start with an underarm throw initiation

-> A pass / volley to your target and passing back 

-> everytime the ball goes higher than the antenna you score a point Keeping the cues in mind for volleying, so high arms, and push with legs (may look to use, left, right footwork but not enforcing just as a potential other cue) 

# Activity 2; Partner passing in 2's Now using your forearms, go through the technique of using your hips and shoulder drop when doing so
-> an underarm throw, straight and the pass going cross court

-> passing with each other behind cones and aiming for higher passes to each other 

-> 3 minutes or 7 consecutive touches (whichever comes first) 

# Activity 2.1 Making it cross court 
In your 2's and behind the cones still we're going to throw the ball and use your forearm pass to go cross to the other player in the other cone 

# Activity 3; Playing 2 vs 2 cross court
-> an underarm throw over 

-> a pass to the setter (looking at the technical cues from activity 1) And a pass back over the net, if it's above the top of the antenna scoring an extra point 

# Game
Look at using the pass being higher than antenna, and a setter locked into a certain part of the court using the cones
