# Building an attack and hit scenario

# resources
- 12 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court


# Warmup
## 1. Groups of 3
 - 1 player at position 2 (as if setting at the net), 1 player at position 4 as if they're an outside, 1 player off the net as a defender
 - A pass to defender, defender to setter, setter to pass out to 4
 - try to keep it continious

## 2. Groups of 3
- Building off the previous drill, now back setting with a player at 2 and a half and a player at 2

## 3. Volleysquash

In pairs, hitting against a wall you get 1 bounce and can decide your shot to try and keep your partner from getting the ball

Look at creating a slow to fast armswing and building a ready position for your attack

## 3.1 Adding a jump into volley squash
- Hit it to attempt to be hard enough to add a 2 step jump, looking at being explosive and slow to fast

# Activity 1 - Catch at peak
Groups of 3.

Setter at net.
Attacker starts off net.
Feeder tosses to setter.
Setter sets.
Attacker uses as many steps as they want, just that the last 2 are to be explosive and fast then catch at maximum reach.

# Activity 2 - Adding the actual attack in
Same groups.

Player starts in defensive position.
Defends a tossed ball.
Transitions off the net.
Setter sets.
Attacker catches or attacks.

# Activity 3 - Now playing the rally out
- 3 vs 3 half court game
- Ball throw in, either a catch or a live pass
- 1 hitter front row

# Game 
Playing 5 vs 5 or 6 vs 6
