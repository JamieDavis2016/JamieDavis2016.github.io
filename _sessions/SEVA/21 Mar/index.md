# Serve receive and tempo
- Players - 11?

- Balls - 6+

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30


# Focus 
2 focuses;

serve receive; Looking at how we can use the court space for serving and where our receive is,
Building on tempo; looking at how we can run the tempo that was discussed last session

# Warmup
- Strength and conditioning (they lead themselves)

- 6 vs 6, Touch and go, when the ball is played
  - Play within the 3m line
  - Everytime the ball is contacted the person that contacts has to go to the endline and return to a different spot on court

Playing to 15 points

# Activity 1 - Over hand contact
- In pairs, from net to endline, a throw or a controlled hit at the partners shoulders or higher
- Defender to pass it with their hands up high
  - Small technical piece on what to do with your hands

# Activity 2 - Serve Receive
- 6 vs 4
  6 on one side of the court, with a server - Play the rally out
  

- 4 side has a setter and 3 players
  
  The 4 side starts with serve receive and starts at the 3m line, they drop back to pass before attacking

- We play points to 7 before switching

# Activity 3 - Building on tempo (again)
- 3 vs 3 half court, setters on both sides
 We are playing an outside and a middle running tempo off of each other

The middle does their approach with the setter and the outside does their approach with the middle

We have a rally and playing 3 vs 3 with a live serve, everytime the ball drops we swap who's the outside and who's the middle

# Activity 4 - Running our tempo system again
Our tempo system looks something like this;

Position 4 = 5

Position 3 = 3

Position 2 = 1

+ the tempo
As a few examples

High ball position 4; 53

Fast ball middle; 31

Normal ball oppo; 12

Slide; 22

Back court A is a position 1 (10), B is a pipe and C would be position 5

We are playing with a live serve and then a free ball to the team that wins the point

You have to win both points to win 1 point for the rally, OR if you run one of the tempo combination in the rally (not serve receive), you also win the point regardless of the first point

Playing to 10, the point difference is a punishment of dives
