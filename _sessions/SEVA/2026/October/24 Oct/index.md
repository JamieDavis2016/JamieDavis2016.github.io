# Part 1; Building our serve receive to enable an attack on siding out 50% of the time

# Activity 1; Service game
In 4 groups of (18 atm, so 2 of 4 and 2 of 5)

We are playing a service game relay race

The game is simple, we start on both the same side of the court, you have to serve in, get the ball back to your team member to then serve, IF the team member serves out they cannot go over the other side until everyone has. Once everyone has got across they also need to get back

The losing team has to do a forfeit that the winning team gives (within reason), 3 of something... (3 dives, 3 jumping jacks...)

# Activity 2; Serving passing lines

In the groups again, we can now engage with serving and passing in 2 lines,

Servers can start anywhere from the 3m line and go backwards. We will start with serving down the line

Every rep a passer and server will swap

This is a warmup to getting our passes to target and where we can position on court

Cues; we'll look at where players position and where they start and propose different positions to try (such as being close to the 3m line or further away and coming in)

# Activity 3; 2 vs 2+ half court games
(with current 18, should be 2 vs 2 with 2 extra)

We'll play points of 5 and a ladder type game, if you are at the top of the ladder you get to be the queens of the court, when the games finish we rotate going up and down the ladder (playing a normal game of volleyball)

2 players are in serve receive

If the serve receive side can create a pass, set, attack (front court) they get an extra point

# Activity 5; 4 vs 4 Queen of the court style game

We are now going to play queen of the court 4s, we can hit from anywhere on the court

Our setter is at position 1 (so back court) and has to rotate in and around

We'll play with a server over and then play the serve receive out

Playing to points of 10 before swapping in the ladder like game

# Activity 2; 1 vs 1 over the net (1/3rd of the court)
We have a setter on both sides, with an attacker every 5 points we rotate the attacker and setter

A free ball thrown over and we're after creating that hand contact again, the back 3ms of the court are in

# Activity 3; 2 vs 2 with our serve receive again

Now looking at bringing the serve receive back

We play a 2 vs 2 game, where the in bounds part of the court is the back 3m of the court

If we generate topspin into our attack, and it's in the zone, you gain 2 points (playing to 5 points on our ladder type system)

# Activity 4; 6 vs 6 with setter at position 1 (rotation 1)
Repeating activity 6 from earlier but now with a focus on the attacking side

Playing to points of 5 so not quite queen of the court, before changing in our ladder game as before (scram to get on court again)

The back 3ms of the court will again gain an extra point
