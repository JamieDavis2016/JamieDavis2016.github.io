# Creating an attack approach from serve receive

# Focus
Creating an attack approach from serve and serve receive, using a 3 step approach L-R-L for right handed players and R-L-R for left handed players (or slow to fast)

# Resources
- 8+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup - Throws and passes
1. Arm throws over the net with your partner, pass to self, using your arm swing mechanics

2. Full attack approach jumps (looking at 3 / 4 step approaches)
  - Variations, 2 step, 3 step, 4 step and running to the net

3. 1 vs 1, 3 person pepper, 1 player at the net and 1 on each side, a pass to the setter at the net, set back and attack, the 1 at the net goes under to try and play the other ball, start with no jump then add in an attack approach

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2, rythumn to your attack
A free-ball over the net (arm throw)

Pass to your partner
Scoring if you have an attack approach (with a jump)

Playing the rally out to score another point

Cues: Passers / setters, double height of antenna

Attackers: slow to fast, tracking the ball and going, be aggressive!

# Activity 2 - Setting up our attack
- 2 setters on other sides of the net
- 2 attackers
- 2 passers

- A ball is thrown from your setter to your passer
- Passer is to pass back to the setter
- Setter sets for the attacker

- Attacker to work on a step approach and a jump

Cues: wait before you go
how do you know when and where to go (what's our first step for?)

# Activity 3 - 3 vs 3 cross court
Players at positions 2, 4 and 5, everywhere else is out of bounds

Starting with a free ball throw in, a pass to the setter and attempting to attack the ball with a jump

(Playing the rally out)

Cues: 
Low in defence
Slow to fast on our attack

## Activity 3.1 - Adding in a serve
Continuing the game but with a controlled serve now

# Game
Every approach attack you score a point
