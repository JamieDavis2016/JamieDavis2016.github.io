# Focus

And having a setter start from back court (looking at running in to play the ball)

- 10+ balls

# Warmup


1. 2 person pepper, with a pass and a set

2. Passing practice,
  - 2 passers on 1 side of the court
  - 2 servers on the other
  - 2 targets
  - 2 attackers (catching to start)

Cues: Early angle, Early platform

# Activity 1 - Run in setter
- Talk about the serving rules, and the setter starting at position 1.

Run in setter, (2 servers) served ball in, if serve misses 1 player throws a ball in, 3 passers, the passer at 5 is also a hitter at 4

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 3 
- Half court game, continuation from last week, the setter is going to start back court (behind the 2 defenders)
- The 4 has 3 defenders

- Serving and executing an attack over the net
  - Our passers are after those same cues, we're looking for double the height of the antenna
  - Our setter is looking to achieve the same thing for our attacker
  - Our attacker is looking for explosion with a L-R-L (left-right-left)  

Cues: Keeping that slow to fast on our attacker, Early platform and angle for defenders

# Activity 3 - Serve and serve receive (7 players?)
- Practicing as a 5 vs 2 (middle is missing) and setter is starting at position 1

- Served ball in, with a free-ball after.
After our pass being high and creating an attacking opportunity
Starting our defence shallow (maybe 60cm  of the 3m line)


# Game
Every ball that's 2x the height of the antenna is a point
