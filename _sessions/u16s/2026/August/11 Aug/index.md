# Focus
Building on the attack approach, we are going to look at creating a high pass, through the volley or forearm pass

# Resources
- 6+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup - Decomposition
1. Shoulders over knees, knees over toes, passing with a partner
 - 10 balls straight
 - 10 balls to the side (where we can shuffle and move)

All of the passes are double the height of the antenna

Cues: Shoulders over knees, knees over toes

3. Full attack approach jumps (looking at 3 / 4 step approaches)
  - Variations, 2 step, 3 step, 4 step and running to the net

4. Brazilian Volleytennis (movement warmup)
 - 1 vs 1, full court, can dig or volley

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2
- Working from a free-ball over

- Pass to your setter, working with the passing cues from before

- The set and forearm pass, we're aiming for double the height of the antenna

# Activity 2 - Serve and serve receive
- 1 server
- 3 passers
- 1 setter
- 1 attacker

A service over,
3 passers to work on pass to setter
(keeping this cue: Shoulders over knees, knees over toes) and adding another, exploding to the ball

Setter to pass out to our attacker

Attacker to work on their approach
Same cues: slow to fast, L-R-L (only decomp in this)

# Activity 3 - 3 vs 3 cross court
- Service ball in
Going over the attack line rule and how we jump before it.

Can attack from anywhere. (lets aim for the ball to be set to the 3m line, otherwise known as an out of system pass)

Cues: Reading the serve and the attack. Low in defence

## Activity 3.1 - Adding some extra constraints
Back court only attacking, with the addition of whoever takes the first ball, we want the attackers to be on the wing of them, so if the player on the right takes the set, the player in the middle runs around to attack

# Game
Every approach attack you score a point
