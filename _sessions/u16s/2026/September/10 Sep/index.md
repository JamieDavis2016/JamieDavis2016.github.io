# Setting up a 4:2 system

What is a 4:2 system?

# Focus
Over the next 4 weeks, introduce and develop a 4:2 system, with players beginning to understand their rotational role and the responsibilities of the setter, outside and middle positions

# Resources
- 14+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup

## Warmup 1; Pairs volleying and digging
### Purpose; 10 minutes of getting our bodies used to some setting and forearm passing techniques
- In groups of 3, volleying to one player the player that didn't volley swaps with someone else, this is to introduce some volleying technique and communication (we'll add the communication piece into this such as, "name, switch!")
 
- 1.1 Adding in the forearm pass now and how we want that to work (using shoulders)
- 1.2 : Now ways to get behind the ball so adding in movement
Cues; Engaging your shoulders in the pass and getting behind the ball

### Warmup 2, Serving game;
2 lines of servers

It's a race to get all the other servers to the other line first and back again!

Once a server gets the ball over and into the court, they can help get the ball back to their teammates

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half courts
Served ball in with another player waiting to send a ball in if the serve misses (1 group of 4 on each half court rotating in)

Cues: Serve receive getting behind the ball (an analogy; We are at a tug of war with each other, the server wants to stop a good pass and the serve receive is fighting to get a good pass)
Other cues: what are our hands doing on the volley?

# Activity 2 - 6 vs 6 - Creating our 4:2 system
## Purpose; Having a system in place so we can get familiar with roles of the game
Quick explanation of positions on the court, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and where they are

With both of these games, 2 players waiting off with a ball to send in if the serve is out or misses

Looking at how our 4:2 system works, designating 2 setters for the game (and this is something we can change)

We're looking at just where the setter will be going on the court for this starting rotational game

## Activity 2.1 - What are we doing when the setter takes the 2nd ball?
- As hitters and defenders we have roles now as well

What are they?

* note:* As a hitter we might be getting ready to attack, as a defender we may be getting ready to move and cover or defend an attack
