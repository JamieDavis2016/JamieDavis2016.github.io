# Warmup 1; Float serves with your partner
### Purpose; Just getting familiar with what a float serve is, what it looks like and how to do it

(short demo on the how)

In pairs over the net at the 3m line or slightly behind and just working on a float serve contact, when you feel more confident you can go backwards

If we want we can pass it to ourselves catch and return

# Warmup 2; Balls in to pass, rotate 
### Purpose; Single person passing technique
In 2 lines working on our passing platform and aiming our serves, starting in and coming back to the ends of the court

1 line is serving line from 1 and another line serving to 5

Having 2 targets to pass to, return to server and rotate round

Cues; Early platform, early angle 

## note;
2 weeks ago we worked on our setter rotating in a 4:2 system, where they are always going over to the right front of the court.
Now we're going to introduce the middle and outside going to their points on court

# Activity 1: - 3 vs 3, half courts
### Purpose; Quick games playing with a middle or outside attacker on one side

On one half of the court there wil be 2 attackers that will be the outside and middle

Then on the other have so the courts are cross from each other the same
The two courts remaining have 3 players playing with a setter front court and 1 hitter front court (doing this for safety)

# Activity 2 - Playing a 4 vs 4 full court game and rotating round
### Purpose; Getting used to rotational play with a 4:2 system

we're going to have our setter, middle an outside and a back court player.

The setter is going to start at position 4 and rotate through, the outside and middle are now going to switch as well

We'll play this queen of the court style, so the team that wins goes through the next team comes onto play against this system, before each team goes on, discuss who is going where (I.E I'm playing setter, outside or middle)

# Gameplay
Playing the 4:2 system with outside and middle now rotating with your setters as well
