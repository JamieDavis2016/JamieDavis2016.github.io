# Attacking, slow to fast

# resources
- 7 / 8 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 2 hours
- 2 courts

# Focus
Looking at creating slow to fast hitting approach rhythm, attempting to use this as an attack 80% of the time

# Warmup
We are going to play a 1 vs 1, 3 courts (1 court split into 3, 2 courts into 6 total)

- A serve in
- Pass to yourself, set to yourself and an attempt at attacking
- Must be a hit, jumping or not

# Activity 1 - Float service practice
- Going through a left step, toss, right, left, attack / float
- Just from the end line, experimenting with how we make a float attack happen

- Looking at experimenting with the slow to fast approach,
- The left step and toss can be slow going faster into the left, right (and vice versa for left handed players)

# Activity 2 - self toss attack
- Passing to a setter and having an attack as our intiation of the game

- If we are unsure how to do this, we'll start without the ball and introduce it later

- When we go to attack, we are looking at a pattern of footwork, but most importantly a slow to fast approach with our footwork and with our arm swing

- Taking it in turns who attacks and that is starting the rally for a 3 vs 3, anywhere on court, no blockers on this

# Activity 3 - Back court attacking
We are going to play a 3 vs 3 game, with a setter at position 2
And a free ball being initiated in

The goal is to create an attack from behind the 3m line with an attack approach and you cannot score if you go over the attack line or if you don't attack the ball


# Activity 4 - 3 vs 3 cross - court
- Playing with zones 1 and 2 being out of bounds
- Zones 3, 4, 5 and 6 in bounds

The initiation is going to be a downball from a player, to the opposition, aiming at cross court, varying the throw and attack
- Starting without a jump
- trying with a jump

We are looking at creating a slow to fast tempo, with our armswing and with our attack

Once the ball is initiated, we are going to try and play the rally out, rotating around after the rally

You can only score a point if the ball is an attack, deeper than the 3m line
