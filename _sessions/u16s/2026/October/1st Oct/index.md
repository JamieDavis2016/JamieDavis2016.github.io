# Serve receive and transition into defence (regardless of position)

# Focus; Developing our serve receive and recognising the transition from serve receive into defence/attack, regardless of position.
## Secondary focus;

Can we use our 3-step attack approach when transitioning to attack?

# Resources
- 8+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Brazillian Volleytennis
### Purpose; Movement, activation warmup
We are competeing with a 1 ball touch over the net, trying to play the 1 touch as fast as we can and rotating around after every touch

Whenever the ball hits the floor that person is out for the rest of the round

# Warmup 2; Working on some attack approaches 
### Purpose: A quick look at our 3 step attack approach with a thrown ball

We are going to look at a 3 step approach, l-r-l with our locator step being on the 3m attack line and getting into a big last 2 steps

In pairs a ball thrown and an attack to start

# Activity 1: 2 vs 2 play
### Purpose; Looking at our serve receive and passing the ball forward
We are now creating a seperation and transition between our serve receive and our defence (these are two different segments of play)

Playing for a 3 touch game, creating an attack front court, using our attacking footwork as well

# Activity 2: 3m court game
### Purpose; serve receive transition into fast defence

We are playing a serve receive game behind the 3m line and then everyone getting inside the 3m line to play the ball (creating a the same footwork on our attack approach)

Once serve received has happend the court shrinks to the 3m attack zone and we are playing a small but fast paced game

bonus points if you remember how to do a float serve

# Activity 3: Cross Court game 
### Purpose; Creating the serve receive transition and defence in our game
Position 2, 4, 5 and 6 are in play 1 and 3 are out

Cross court game where we now have a serve receive, our hitter at position 4 comes back into the serve receive then plays up front to attack
