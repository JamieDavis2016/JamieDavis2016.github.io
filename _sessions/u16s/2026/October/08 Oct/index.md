# Warmup 1; Balls in to pass, rotate 
### Purpose; Single person passing technique
In 2 lines working on our passing platform and aiming our serves, starting in and coming back to the ends of the court

1 line is serving line from 1 and another line serving to 5

Having 2 targets to pass to, return to server and rotate round

Cues; Can we get there in as few steps as possible?
Getting behind the ball

# Warmup 2; Working on some attack approaches 
### Purpose: A quick look at our 3 step attack approach with a thrown ball

We are going to look at a 3 step approach, l-r-l with our locator step being on the 3m attack line and getting into a big last 2 steps

In pairs a ball thrown and an attack to start

# Activity 1: 3 vs 3 half courts
### Purpose; Can our serve receivers get a pass to our setter using what we did from warmup1?

Setter is front court with an attacker, 1 defender behind

We're attempting to get our serve receive to our setter

Our setter is putting a high ball and creating a transition for our attacker to have a 3 step L-R-L approach

# Activity 2: 4 vs 4, Rotating around
### Purpose; Serve receive work with our setter

Rotating like a normal game of volleyball

Setter is front court, we have an outside attacker that plays in the serve receive

When the attacker goes to rotate they become the setter, then we rotate off

# Activity 3: Introducing a faster attack
Showing our serve receive, to a middle attack (and looking at what's called tempo)

On a half court, a ball thrown in for a defender, our attacker is then going to work with the setter to try attempt a faster attack (being on the L-R of their approach)

Doing this on 2 half courts with 2 rows of people behind each other

### Note;
Go through our serve receive rotations again (see if we are understanding this) for our 4:2 system and what the overlap rule means
