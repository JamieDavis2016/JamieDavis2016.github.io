# Activity 1: 3 vs 3 half courts
### Purpose; Working on our serve receive positioning, as well as what our left seam looks like

Setter is front court with an attacker, 1 defender behind

We're attempting to get our serve receive to our setter

Our setter is putting a high ball and creating a transition for our attacker to have a 3 step L-R-L approach

Our serve receive is going to start at the 3m line and drop back

We're after 2 front and 1 back court

(draw a diagram of our left seam)

Cues; Reading the server and dropping into position 

left arm in front, right arm behind, to show our left seam

# Activity 2: 4 vs 4, Rotating around
### Purpose; Serve receive work with our setter

Rotating like a normal game of volleyball

Setter is front court, we have an outside attacker that plays in the serve receive

When the attacker goes to rotate they become the setter, then we rotate off
