# Building on serve receive and roles / responsibilities

# resources
- 10 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 / 2 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 1 courts

# Focus
Building an idea of how serve receive works with the rules that was shown last week

# Warmup - Ladder game
2 vs 2 half court, serve in, with a 3 touch rally, can only score if the set is inside the attack line

Starting with a standing attack and then moving onto a jump attack (looking at an approach, left, right)

Playing a best of 3 (with cones at the back to knock out the opponent instantly)

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half court
Setter is at position 1 on 1 side and setter is at position 2 on the other

- Service in and an attack from inside the attack line


# Activity 2 - 6 vs 4
- Setter is starting at position 1 on the 4 side, with service coming from the 6

- (Setter swaps to be position 2), every 7 points
- Now lets add in who wants to play as a middle blocker, and an outside attacker, on the 6 side

On the 4 side, we have serve receive into an attack, the set has to be anywhere in the attack zone

If you're out of rotation you lose 2 points

# Activity 3 - 5 vs 5
- After every serve, we have a different setter starting at position 1

ball in from 1 team if the rally ends quickly after the service, with a setter back court taking that first ball. We now have to still make an attack happen over the net (from the attack zone)

# Game
Normal 5 vs 5, bonus points if they set positions with a middle, outside and a setter
