Building our serve and serve receive unit, how to float serve, building on our passing and our seams

- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour

# Warmup - Throws and passes
1. -> In pairs using the net across from each other

Ball thrown over the net as if you're serving or attacking

We then self pass, catch and return the ball

2. Starting Float service
Cues: strong open hand, throw the ball up and contact with palm (fingers not touching)

# Activity 1 - 2 person pass on a half court
Using half a court, 2 passers, 1 target and 1 attacker on one side (and the same on the other)

Passers at positions 5 and 6

- Starting with resistance bands on the passers
- Serves are ball throws at the moment
- Attacker tries an attack approach catching at the top
- Draw smiley faces on passers

Cues: Smiley faces to target, Get to the ball being explosive (I.E shuffle with explosion) and working on the seam together

## 1.2 - Adding a float serve in
No longer ball throws but serves

## 1.3 - Adding an attack
- Using your attack approach and changing

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3 half courts
- Playing the rally out 

- Scoring 2 points for an effective pass

- Scoring 3 points for an ace

To Scores of 15


# Activity 3 - 3 before 5
Servers versus passers game
3 passers, 2 servers 

Scoring a 2 or a 3 pass (on receive) is 1 point 

Passers have to get 5 points before servers get 3

Servers score off of a 1 pass or ace

Lastly, you are playing as a team on one side, so the passers and servers on one side of the court are a team together

Cues: working on the seam together

# Game
5 vs 5 / 6 vs 6

- Extra point for a 3 pass in receive
