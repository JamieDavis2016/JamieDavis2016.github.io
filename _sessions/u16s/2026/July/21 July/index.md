# Handling serve receive

# Focus
Handling a serve receive pass and creating an attack opportunity from the middle of the court, getting that pass to the middle 50% of the time

# Resources
- 6 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup
Body weight squats
Approach jump plyos

15 self passes with both arms
15 left arm, 15 right arm

In pairs throwing the ball over the net (like you would attack)

A self pass to a catch and throw back (looking at passing technique)

# Activity 1 - Four corners
A throw in from 3m line

2 back court, 2 front court
The initial passer has to take the first ball to the middle of the court

Second passer to push it out to the front row attacker, the attacker does an approach and catches

Rotating through, the setter becomes attacker, attacker replaces the ball and joins the queue

Cues: Quick to the ball on the pass (passer) shoulders forward creating a platform, 
Getting behind the ball (setter)
Exploding to attack (attacker)

# Activity 1.2  - Adding in an attack
Building on the throw in we now attack the ball instead of catch

# Activity 1.3 - Adding in a serve
The coach is removed as a thrower, the attacker becomes a server

And we are trying to work on building a pass from the serve receive

# Activity 2 - Serve Receive and Serve
3 players to pass, a target and 2 servers

Serving from 3m line and stepping back

On the serve receive side, we'll talk about seam responsibility and keeping with the cues: Quick to the ball on the pass (passer) shoulders forward creating a platform

# Activity 3 - 3 vs 3, cross court
Playing with a serve in, and 2 passers, on a half court game

Playing at positions 3, 4 and 5
We're aiming for a ball to be executed

# Game
The 3 vs 3 cross court, but with a score

Scoring 3 points for an attack that wins the point
Scoring 2 points for a serve receive pass that goes to target
Scoring 1 point as a normal game would
