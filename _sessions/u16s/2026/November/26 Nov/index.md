# Movement based reading

# Focus: Movement based on reading the game and what you see, getting it right 50% of the time

# Resources;
- 13+ players
- 10+ balls
- 2 coaches
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court


# Warmup;
1. Volleytennis in 2's

Getting movement into our game and trying to controllably get the ball over, playing our usual game into our knockout stage

2. 2 vs 2 / 3 vs 3
Playing our warmup game of dig, set, volley
and dig, set, attack (can be standing and jumping when warm)

We are after a block and playing these small sided rallies out

# Movement / diving
1. We are going to go through what's none as a split step, keeping our stability with the ground being our friend

So an initial going through what a split step is

And then in pairs, having the ball thrown from the chest, going into a split step and passing it back / up

2. Going through how to dive to the side or forward and teaching to land forward or sideways.

Starting with a forwards dive into sliding forward with our chest

Then in pairs having the ball thrown low and trying to get to the ball with a dive (can start on our knees or low enough that it doesn't hurt to land)

Then going through how to dive to the sides, emphasising landing on the sides of our legs rather than smacking knees

Then in pairs having the ball thrown low and trying to get to the ball with a dive (can start on our knees or low enough that it doesn't hurt to land)

# Activity 1; Drill of 15 (4s)
Players at 4, 2, 5 and 6

A free ball starting in and we are trying to play a continuous game of 15 touches, we rotate everytime the ball goes over the net so have to move quickly!

dig, set, volley - Initiation: freeball

dig, set, standing attack - Initiation: freeball

dig, set, jumping attack - Initiation: downball

# Activity 2; How fast can the ball go over?

In our 4s and rotating round still

We are going to create how fast can the ball come over in our rallies?

So the first ball over means

On the initiation (freeball), we are sending a pass straight over to the opposition

On the second ball over

On the initiation (freeball), we are passing to our team and sending it over, however it can also go over on the first touch (as long as it's deliberate)

On the third ball over

On the initiation (freeball), we are passing to our team and sending it over. Can be through an attack or anyway. however it can also go over on the first touch, or second (as long as it's deliberate)

# Scrimmage
Playing our games now watching movement
