# Approach and hand contact / defensive posture

# Pre-requisites
9 balls, 16 players, 1 net 

# Focus
approach / hand contact in our attack, palm hitting the ball 50% of the time and in defence staying low and ready, not letting the ball drop without a touch

# Warmup
3 vs 3 1 at the net, rotating 1 in each time 
- No jump, hitting deep into the back of the court
- Progress to jump and blocker, blocker to get in front of the attacker (blocker is not the setter) 
- Defenders to be on the sides of the blocker 
  - 2 step approach 
  - Hand open and palm contacting the ball

# Moving onto some technical practice
3 hitters left side and 3 hitters right side with 2 setters 4 blockers on the other side, spliting the block down the middle and 4 defenders

# Putting into practice
4 vs 4 (1 court) 
- Service in
- rotating players round and playing a serve into an attack from back court
- Working on our attack approach (2 / 3 steps) 
  - and hand contact 
- For more advanced to look at hitting the right side of the court (keep away from the libero)
- Score starts at 5 - 5
  - Everytime a player doesn't go for the ball defensively, they lose a point for the team (if goes to 0 punishment), normal scoring to 12

# Our 6 vs 6
 - setter at 1 (back court),
 
  working on our attack and staying out of the way of the block in defence, A service into an attack coming from a wing hitter to initiate the ball, looking at our attack and hand contact
