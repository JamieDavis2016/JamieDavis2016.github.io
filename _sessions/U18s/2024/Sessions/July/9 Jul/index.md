# Focus
All about defence

## Warmup
- Pepper Variation
  - Lib + Setter at net, focus on passing quality
- Hit, dig, hit
- Controlled hit into middle

# Overhead Passing
- 3 front, 2 back
- 3 minutes before swap
- All hands and movement

## Semi Circle Passing
- Semi circle cones, shuffle step to each cone and back
- Out and around and back again

## Right side Passing (5 and 6)
- Shuffle step around, passing a set of rapid fire balls in groups of 3
- 2 minute intervals

### Short / Deep
- Change the ball to be either a short or deep ball
