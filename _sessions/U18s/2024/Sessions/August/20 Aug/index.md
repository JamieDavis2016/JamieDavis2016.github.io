🔥 1. Warm-up (10 minutes)

    Drill: Pepper 2's

        Focus on early platform and early angle setup.

        Coaching Point: Use aggressive throws to simulate the incoming power of a hit.

        Key Technique: Power comes from the hips; the arm should be hidden (cocked) until the last moment before contact.

✋ 2. Free Ball / Platform Work (10 minutes)

    Focus: Perfecting platform technique and footwork for easy-to-handle balls.

    Groups: Groups of 3s or 5s.

    Drill: Discuss platform control on fast vs. hard balls.

        Throw for speed, requiring players to move around the court.

        Focus on setting the platform up and angled at the target (Setter/target zone).

        Emphasize using a push with the legs to direct the ball, not just the arms.

🎯 3. Over the Net (20-25 minutes)

    Format: Half court. 1 minute on the timer per repetition/round.

    Groups: Groups of 3, half court.

    Task: Choose to down ball or hit/serve (depending on the drill).

    Drill: Teams that are up/win can do steer ball practice (controlled tipping/placement).

🛡️ 4. Serve vs Hit/Pass (10 minutes)

    Format: Half court.

    Groups: Groups of 3.

    Rotation/Flow:

        Setters to 10 passes (perfect serve-receive passes).

        Then, pass and hit to 6 (perfect rallies/hits).

        The Passer is also the hitter for repetition.

        The Setter sets (self-explanatory).

⛏️ 5. Dig a Dozen (10 minutes)

    Focus: Defensive diving and digging reps.

    Goal: Successfully dig 12 balls in a row or within a set time limit.

🏆 6. Game (Time Permitting)

    Application: Put the skills learned into a full-court, competitive game setting.
