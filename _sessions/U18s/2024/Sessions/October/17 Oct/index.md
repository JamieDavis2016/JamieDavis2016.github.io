# Focus
Looking at how we cover our attackers

# Warmup
- Dancings hands
- Shoulder taps
- On pepper go line to line, generating topspin on your hit and control (hitting slightly underneath the ball and following through)

1. Setter at the net, tight balls and sets
2. Sets to 4, 2, B / push
3. Read and run watch body, 3 on court find gaps and talks (Jim Stone's defensive video on court coverage)

# Cover drill
- Hitters tip back, short or strong and deep (attackers on 1side, attack backwards)
- 3 blockers on other side of net
- 2 - 3 pattern
  - 3 back off 3m, 2 cover short
- 4 contacts to reset

# Play rally out
Playing set hits and other team has to read the attack and adapt off of it
