# Focus
Looking to have some fun with this session as we just played our first tournament

# Warmup
- Split groups with who prefers cats or dogs (this was not an even split in the slightest)
- Pepper with who likes the same group
- High five hand game
- 1 arm defence, 1 arm, pass, drop back to defence, to handle an attack from passer

# Ball starts at the net
- 1 vs 1, 1 arm pass, play it off the net

# Level game
- 3 players on horses (or stable higher surfaces), have to return an attack to dethrown them, a tap from the attacker initiates the game and they have to get into defensive posture, 4 on court, 1 setter 3 defenders (that can attack front court)


Level 1;
Everyone starts off the court 

Level 2; Everyone comes into the middle (hands in / touching)

Level 3; Everyons has to start in the corner of the court

# Middle and pipe tracking 
- Ball over, tracking and getting ready to hit

# Game
First to 25
