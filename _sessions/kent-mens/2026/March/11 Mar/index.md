# Back court coverage (mainly positions 5 and 6)
- Players - 12

- Balls - 6+

- 1 court 

- 2 hours


# Focus 
Looking at back court coverage, what are our roles? Can we get a pass even if it's 20% of the time from any attack

# Activity 1 (warmup) - 3 vs 3
2 front and 1 back

Warming up aiming for attacks to be hitting in the last 3m of the court

Start with no jump

Once jumps is added in, wanting the ball to the net as a front court attack (hitting deep)

# Activity 2 - Looking for where's open on court
On one side 5 players receiving side
- Play serve receive and an attack (with rally)

On serving side 6 players, that start on service

Going to start with spread blocking (going 1 to 1 with the attacker on the receiving side)

The defence is to look for where's open and defend there first (looking at the blocker)

- 5 points before rotating

# Activity 2.1 - Adding in the middle blocker 
Same drill adding in a 2nd blocker

# Activity 3 - 3 before 7
Serving side has 6 players

Receiving side has 5 players, right back, left back, middle back, left front and right front (when we go to in rally, your setter is left back to start)

The serving side has to defend 3 balls before the receiving side scores 7 points
