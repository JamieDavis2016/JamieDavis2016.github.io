# Building from sideout to build an attack

## Resources
- 12 + players
- 10 + balls
- 1:30 time
- 1 court

# Focus
Using sideout and in rally to create an effective attack, looking at deeper into the court as well as away from position 6

https://www.theartofcoachingvolleyball.com/training-more-efficiently/
(this is a subscription model) - talks about leading the attacker as well as the setter

# Warmup
- 3 vs 3 half court, 3m serve, no jump and playing deeper court shots, we'll add in court rules to show that the attack must be deep
- Adding in the jump and a block

# 4 vs 4
- Full court, live serve game, same court rules on the deep court attack
- 3 front court (setter to start front court)
- Move setter back court, having 3 front court

The passers are going to keep the ball in front of the setter.
Setters are going to lead their footwork, watching the pass, we're then going left, right to the antenna and adjusting our body.

# Cross court pepper
- Serve initiation, positions 1,2,3,4,5 are in, 6 is out, we can have 3 in the receive (4 comes back)
- Changing what's in court to 2,3,4,5,6

We can use our tempo that we've been working on with our outsides and middles (even pipes)

Attackers to keep the ball in front of them

Setters to keep watch of the pass and adapt

Defenders, leading your passers

# Game
Playing out the game, looking at deeper shots and adding a tempo attack possibly
