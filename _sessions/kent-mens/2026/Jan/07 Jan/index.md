First session back after xmas

## Pre-session
- 2 hours (1900 - 2100)
- 6 to 10 balls
- 11 players

# Building our tempo and cross court play

# Focus;
Playing cross court, keeping with the tempo we tried running in the session before, if we can run this as an attack in game awesome

# Warmup
# Exercise 1 - Touch and go
- Playing within the attack zone (3m), anything goes, 3 touches, 2 touches, anything, once the ball has been passed must go to the endline and touch base
- Initiation will be a serve in
- Once built in and warmed up we move it into an attack and further back into the court (still running to touch endline)

# Into our session
## Exercise 2 - 3 vs 3 half court
- Start with a small warmup of it, where we initiate a thrown ball over, build the middle and outside attack (alternating attackers)
- Ball is thrown, pass happens and set, middle is attacking on the setter pass, the outside is starting their approach on the middle jumping

- Moving on;
Initiation is a serve from 3m, a pass to the setter, setter is then going to use the middle or a pipe attack (running that same tempo before)

# Activity 3 - Cross court pepper
- Playing a cross court pepper

Starting with positions 5 and 4 out of bounds, this means we have players at 6, 3, 2 - everyone rotates in after the ball goes over, the attack is going to be either the middle or the pipe again aiming for the tempo

- May start with the attack being a volley push to build up into attacking

- We then change this to be positions 6 and 1 out of bounds,
meaning we use attacks coming from 3 (middle) and 4 (outside), again trying to use the tempo's from the earlier practice

# Activity 4 - Game 
- If we run any tempo like this where the pipe or outside are working off the middle
- Also have new players to introduce to a 5:1 system
