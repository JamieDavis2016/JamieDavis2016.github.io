# Attacking consistency, hitting

# resources
- 15+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1hour 30 mins

# Focus
Looking at back court defence off of our attack adding in their roles and responsibilities

# Warmup
- Volleytennis, 1 touch and over, playing just forearms

# Activity 1 - Error correction
- 3 vs 3, down ball in
rotating round everytime the ball goes over

No jump to start, adding in the jump and attacking behind the 3m line

# Activity 2 - Hitting lines 
- Be doing hitting lines in groups of 3 for 4 minutes each

With live defenders to work off of the attack (and a blocker possibly)

# Activity 3 - Cross Court pepper
- Players at 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6
Cannot hit at 1, hitting at 1 and 5

- Change it to be playing at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6
Hitting at 1 and 6

Aiming for 2 blockers each time

# Game
- Playing it out

If defenders transition out and can defend the ball we get a point
