# Focus 
Ball control and attack selection (aiming for attack to be there 50% of the time)

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play
- double touch from receiver / defender to the attacker (so a pass to self and a set to attacker)

Can start with no jump and once warm lean towards adding a spike in

# Activity 2 - Server vs passer
3 passers, starting inside the attack line, have to read the serve and drop back to pass, a target to give a score 1 to 3

1 being a shanked or unplayable ball
2 being playable
3 being a good pass to the setter

We can add an attack in from target to become a setter and we can attack after the receive (receiving side being 4 and defending side being 6)

# Activity 2.1 - server vs passer
- Passer starts at end line (end of court), looks to come in to play the receive (tracking the ball and trying to play a pass) - scoring of 3

# Activity 3 - Scoring when an attack happens
- Playing a normal live serve game

Setter is at position 1 and everyone rotates into it, you score a point for every attack made

2 points for a ball that wins the rally
and 1 point for a ball attacker must have an approach and attack

3 front and 2 back (unless we get 12)

# Game
Looking at how we react to service pressure and continuing to attempt an attack at least 50% of the time
