# What does our block do?
- Players - 10

- Balls - 6+

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30

# Warmup
- Block touches
  - Step out, 1 step
  - Step out, 1 step, reach
  - Shuffle, 2 step
  - Crossover step, 3 step

## 2 vs 2 / 3 vs 3
- Playing 2 front (on 3s), 1 front on 2s
  - Service in (live server anywhere behind 3m line)
- Front court to try and read the attack, get the block in front of hitters shoulders

# Activity 1 - 4 vs 6 (SR)
Setter on the 4 side is front court, leaving 2 attackers front court (outside and middle) and a pipe / 10 ball

The 6 a side team is always receiving

What are we looking at on our serving side as blockers?
What are we reading? Where is the setter?

What happens to our defence?

(Note: ready position to block, commmitting to the block)

- Playing a game to 7, the winning side gets to choose who changes where

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 6 (SR)
- Continuing with how Activity 1 went
  - Now adding a downball to the 6 side (serving side)

- What changes about or block?
  What happens if the middle doesn't get there?

# Game
- If we are correct on reading the set and block, 1 extra point in the rally
