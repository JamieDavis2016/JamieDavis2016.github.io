# Looking for available spaces on court

# resources
- 7? players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coache
- 1hour 30 mins
- 1 court

# Focus
Looking for available spaces on court

# Activity 1 - It compounds
Starting with a 1 vs 1 thrown ball in,

When the ball is put to the other side an extra player can join, making it a 2 v 1, when the ball comes back over it becomes a 2 vs 2, until everyone is on court

- A throw or a serve from behind the 3m line in to initiate

# Activity 2 - Band passing, In pairs
Ball throws behind and you have to pass with your forearms or volley, with bands around your ankles

This is looking at our shuffle step and creating a solid platform

- 1 side is the server and another player (2)
- other side is a 4 with 2 players having bands as receivers

The 4 side is aiming for a pass from the 2 players with bands, with a set and an attack

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4 full court
- If the player is on the other side of the markers and you score the side they're not on 3 points

- We're looking for where defenders are not or if there's a weaker defender

Starting at 2, 4, 5 and 6

Then swapping to 1, 2, 4 and 6
