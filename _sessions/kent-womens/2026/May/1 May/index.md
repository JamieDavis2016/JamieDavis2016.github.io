# Varying our attack options and picking where's open on the court

# resources
- 10 / 11? players
- 10+ balls
- 1 / 2 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 1 court

# Focus
Looking at how we adapt to various attacks, creating options based on the pass we're given

# Activity 1 - Serve and serve receive
- Using bands for our passing, 4 vs 2, with a live serve and 2 passers on the bands

Having the 4 side, have 2 passers with a setter

the 2 side has a blocker and a server

After 7 points swap

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3 half courts
1 front court attacker, 1 back court attacker and 1 setter

Creating an attackable ball from the pass we're given, questioning what our setter sees and what our attacker is looking at and positioning


# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4 full court
- Playing at positions 2, 4, 5 and 6

If the ball is a good quality pass, we can set below the height of the antenna, if the ball is bad quality (off the net) what becomes are attacking options, front court / back court?
