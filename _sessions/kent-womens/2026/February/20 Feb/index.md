# Working on in and out of system play

# resources
- 9+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1hour 30mins

# Focus
Creating an attack option, when in or out of system, 50% of the time
 - What are we after? Everyone has a role in the system, we play to create an attacker every time

# Warmup
2 vs 2 half court
Serving at endline and no jump attack (servers on one side and receivers on the other)

Communicating mine or go before the ball goes over the net, "mine" or "go"

Rotate through every 3 points to the receiving side from serving
Cannot score unless an attack happens

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 back court
- Full width of the court

Aiming for an attack between you two in 3 touches, from a live serve

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3 (adjusting from activity 1)
- 2 back court and 1 front court
Rotating through win or lose the point

After the 3rd touch to be an attack, to the front row player (can be back row as well but must be deliberate)

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4
- 2 front and 2 back
- Setter can start back court then go front court

We decide on if the setter is taking the 2nd ball

Trying to create an attack to the wings on the 3rd touch

# Game
Working on our out or in system attack

The goal is to take the burden of the setter being the most critical for every ball

If the setter cannot get there, back court steps in to make an attack happen

If we attack from an out of system ball, an extra point is awarded
