# Re-hashing positions

# resources
- 9+ players (ideally 12 would be best here)
- 10+ balls
- 1hour 30mins

# Focus
Rebuilding on positions for those that are learning them still, building on our sideout AND creating every ball to be hittable 90% of the time

- To make the ball hittable we need to lead the attacker

# Warmup
2 vs 2 - 3 touch, starting with a volley on the attack
- The 2nd pass / touch should lead the attacker but around the 3m line (give your attacker room)
- With the volley, make sure we're behind the ball and can push aggressively

Next we add a downball in (same drill)
Lastly adding in a jump

# Activity 1 - First Ball kill
- We're going to play a 6 vs 6 (if 5 we remove just 1 position)
- Playing service and serve receive, we have 1 chance to attack, if the defending side stops the ball hitting the floor, they win the point

# Activity 2 - 3 Service play
- Playing a 6 vs 6 rally
- You have 3 serves before we rotate
- The coach is going to call the rotation at which the setter needs to be on the SR (serve receive) side, the team has 15 seconds to get into positions before play happens

I.E, Setter at position 3 - this would be rotation 5 on the sheet

# Activity 2.1
- If we are aced, their will be a free ball in to keep play active and high (the free ball goes to the aced side)

# Activity 3 - Normal game play
Playing the game and observing if we all understand our rotations
- Seeing that every ball is hittable even if it's a downball
