# Creating opportunities for our block and defense

# resources
- 12+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 1 courts

# Focus
Creating opportunities for our blockers and defense to align with each other, creating a more robust defense

# Warmup - 2 vs 3
- 2 vs 3

- Start without a jump, a serve in from 3m line and an attack in the front of the 3m line (looking at the net), when we feel warm add the jump and attack in
- 1 Person blocks and the other defends on both sides of the court
- Player that's off rotates from the 2 to the 3 and the player off rotates into the 2
- There will be some cones that will score 3 points (putting the other team out instantly) OR a block touch is 1 point, these are the only ways to score

Points of 3 before rotating

# Activity 1 - Small court with a block

3 vs 3, small width court (about 3 arms lengths of the court)

Playing a serve in, with the serving side, there are 2 blockers (which may take up a big portion of the court)

queen of the court style games

How can we play around a block and a court this size? Can we attack fast? Can we attack around or even over the blockers?

# Activity 2 - 5 vs 5
2 front court, 3 back court
A serve, and then a free ball / tip, can go anywhere, rotate in after the serve and (free ball / tip), Our setter is front court

With our 3 back court, look at where's available on the court
and the 2 front court players, have to decide where to block and why?

Playing to 7 points, must get both points to win 1 big point

# Activity 3 - 5 vs 5
2 front court, 3 back court
A serve, and then a free ball / tip, can go anywhere (but will look behind the block), rotate in after the serve and (free ball / tip), Our setter is back court

What changes when our setter is front or back court?

Playing to 7 points, must get both points to win 1 big point

# Game
Get an extra point if we get 2 blockers to the attacked ball
