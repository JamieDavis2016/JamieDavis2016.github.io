# Focus
Building a server receive unit, to enable a good first pass to create an attack opportunity

# Resources
- 10+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Working on a float service contact
### Purpose; how do we creat a float serve? What are some details needed? This is to clarify what a float service is 

In pairs, can be at the 3m line and going back, we're going to work on how to create a float serve and what to look for, for that to happen


## warmup 2; Serving game
### Purpose; Get our serving arms ready and our bodies warm

2 lines of servers

It's a race to get all the other servers to the other line first and back again!

Once a server gets the ball over and into the court, they can help get the ball back to their teammates

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half court
## Purpose; Getting the serve receive reps in with a teammate (little bit of seam work)
- Serving on one side, executing an attack on the serve receive side

The setter is going to start at position 1 (and everyone is going to try it here)
Mix up the setter with who's attacking as well

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 4
Queen of the court style, we're working off just the first ball execution, once the attack has happened if the defending side gets a touch they win the point

(Lets create lines of servers so this is fast paced)

With the setter starting at positions 1, 4 and 5

# 2.1 
Now add in the full rally

# Activity 3 - 6 vs 6, 
## Purpose; Creating our serve receive unit
### Note / question; can we consistently turn serve receive into an organised attack?

- Every 3 serves calling a different rotation, with the rotation being called it's going to be where the setter should be

You have 10 seconds to figure it out before the ball gets played, if the positions are incorrect will give a point to the opposition

Playing to points of 11

Cues; What are we looking for as servers? Are we seeing gaps in the serve receive? What about where the setter is?

# Gameplay
We will play our normal game to points of 11. Then we will change positions of players, for example a player wanting to play outside is playing middle will swap with the middle and the outside
