# What we have
- 4U16s, 7U18s
- 1 court and 20+ balls

# Warmup (20mins)
Standing throw looking at defence
  - Getting stance big and body ready (posture)
  - Start in 2's, then 3's with a target
    - Add in an attack

# Half court games
- Playing 2v2 for U16s and 3 vs 3 for U18s
  - Change with person that made a mistake, quick throws into keep the ball being played, ensuring we're after a jump and hit as well as that low defensive posture is happening

# Cross court pepper
- Positions 5, 4, 2 and a block


Start with a pass at 5 to the setter and player at 4 plays over with a pass, looking to get 15 touches before we move on
- Then make it a standing hit
- Then jumping hit
  - Watching defensive movement, look for prepared body (posture)

# Game
Extra rules, jump and kill = 2 points
If your defensive posture is ready negate the 2 points to just a regular point
First to 25
