We also got promoted to the silver cup for our last GP #5!

# Warmup (20mins)
Ball control drill 15 reps before moving onto the next one and if the ball drops restart from the start 
- 4 minutes to complete all of them
- Starting with volley and only 
 - Forearm only
 - Jump set
 - 2 contact and forearms pass
- Narrow lanes (passing drill)

# One side can score
- 3 person teams, 3 minutes each, free ball in
- 1 point for dig, 1 point for winning the rally
- Anything goes

# Skill Development; Posture (20mins)
- 6 up
- Everyone low on the ball except the player that peels off (other side of wing)
- Low, legs should be shoulder width to slightly more apart
- Peeler to be in moderate posture (not as low)

# Tactic Developmnet; bunch read blocking (20 mins)
- Free ball, down ball, 6 on court
- Outside to middle is elbows (width apart)
- Oppo to middle is finger tips (width apart)

We're trying to get 4 hands to every ball

# Game
First to 25 
- Bunch block 1 point
- low and off the cover 1 point
Wash with a downball
