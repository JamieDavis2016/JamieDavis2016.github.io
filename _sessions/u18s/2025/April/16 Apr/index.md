# Warmup (20mins)
- Under and over the net
  - side to side
- 3 vs 3, throw to start, no jump
  - Cross Court

# Technical (30)
- Throw butterfly drill
  - Pass the thrown ball with a freeze to target - throw around and movement (5 each and move on)
- Hit from 3m (5 each and move on)
- Throw into a swing

# Tactical / games (20)
- 1 side 3 front, 1 back
  - 6 on other side, f ree ball to offence from defence 2 touch over to defence side (out of system)

- Setter from 1
  - Lib can choose to rotate to 5 or bump

First to 25
