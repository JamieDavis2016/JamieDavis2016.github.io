# Warmup (20mins)
- 2 vs 2 game, catch once per side
  - 1 contact, 2 contact, 3 contacts

# Technical (30)
- 2 balls, 2 players, volley and shuffle, outside leg in front, line shot
  - adjust to a cross shot
- 1 side hitting lines, with a perfect pass, players at 1 and 5, defenders to get 4 before hitters get 6
  - Same drill but no jump and an attack, cones to emphasise approach 
- Round 1 cone, short and deep

# Tactical / games (20)
To the ball bumping, 3 touches (cross court), positions 4, 5 and 6 - cooperative till 6 touches then a hit.

5 vs 5 back court passers (2 front, 3 back)

4vs 4 / 1 rotate whoever makes the mistake
- serve into a free ball over, if pass isn't perfect, cannot play
1 blocker to get to the ball, everyone drop off

# Game
First to 25
