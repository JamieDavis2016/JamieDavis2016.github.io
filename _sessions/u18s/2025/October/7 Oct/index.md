# Focus;
Consistent hittable balls out to the pipes / wings with height double the antenna
## Sub goal;
Overpass = lost point (so stop overpassing)

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
- Double height of antenna into an attack
  - Ball throw and clap, clap at height and drop then do it with eyes shut
- Queen of the court
- 3vs 3 / 4 vs 3 (setter on 1 side)
  - Ball out to antenna (noodles set for limits on inside of court)

# Hitting lines 
- 2 feeders, 2 setters, 2 passers, 3 hitters
  - 3 minute cycles
- Starting on the net, getting off the net
- L, R, L footwork
- With 3 in defence / base positions, looking at transitions

# Free ball sent from opposition over
 - Where to place the ball, being low and ready
 
# Game 
First to 25
