# Focus;
Steady controlle pass with a freeze to target, enabling a high ball 50% of the time

## Sub goal;
Defensive positioning in rally when dealing with all types of attackers

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
In pairs 8 throws swap
- Toss easy ball freeze platform to target
- Ball thrown deep at them, shuffle to side freeze platform
- All throw to the right (then to the left), step cross, hop (on tap of the ball from feeder)
- Add a hit on all

# Defensive transition (without a ball)
- Going through transitioning on every type of attack
  - Outside, transitions from everyone on that type of attack
  - Middle, transitions from everyone on that type of attack
  - Opposite, transitions from everyone on that type of attack

# 3 vs 3, 2 in pass
 - Initiation must have 1 of the warmups (I.E an attack to the right of the defenders, over the net)
 - Middle and pipe attacks
 - Low defence
 
# Game 
- 60% sideout
- at least 1 rally back seeing defence in play
- looking at freezing to target on the pass
First to 25
