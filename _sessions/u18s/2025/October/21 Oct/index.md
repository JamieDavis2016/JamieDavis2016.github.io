# Focus;
Serving deep with a float serve, receive to be strong enough to play middle
## Sub goal;
Going on the sides of the court for service (more advanced)

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
- Pepper variation / 6 up - freezing to target
- Setter ball handling for Middle / opposite

# Serving hoops (deep court)
  - using hoops on court, servers have to get it into the hoop, then go stand in hoop they can catch
- A race between the two teams and have to get everyone into the hoops first
- Hoops being put deep into court as target
  - Starting with servers at 3m (focus on contact) and then endline

# Working on toss and contact (kneeling)
- Then end line at positions 1 / 5 (standing)

# One ball kill - Deep serve
Playing a rally out where the first attack ends the rally, if defence get a touch they win the point and trying to target deep serves into the court
- SR and an attack on these deeper serves
  - Sub goal; defensive positions
  - Good pass to use the middle

# Broken Wheel at R1 (credit to John)
- Only 1 side rotates, otherside is not rotating at rotation 1
- Working on reception to get stronger
