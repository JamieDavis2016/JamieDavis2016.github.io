# Focus;
Playing continious cross court with high balls and an approached attack
## Sub goal;
Advanced players, aim at 5, killing with no touch from defensive side

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
Jump approaches
- Hand contact, wrist snap
- Pepper with intent of cross swing (in 3's / 4's)

# 3 vs 3 half court, cross court
- Playing in half courts, going cross
- Noddles for 4 and 2 (where we can attack from)

# Hitting lines 
- 2 feeders, 2 setters, 2 passers, 3 hitters
  - 3 minute cycles
- Starting on the net, getting off the net
- L, R, L footwork
- With 3 in defence / base positions, looking at transitions
- 1 blocker on both sides

## Adjustment on the hitting lines
- The pass now is going to 5, 5 taking the first pass, 6 taking the 2nd pass, putting it towards 4 or 2 (front court)

# Continious pepper cross court
- 2, 4, 5, 6 are in play
  - Base defence then out
  - If 6 takes 2nd ball out to 4 on antenna
  
# Game 
First to 25
