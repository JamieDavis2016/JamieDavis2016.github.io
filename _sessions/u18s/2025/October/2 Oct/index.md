# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
- Block warmup
  - Step jump
  - Side shuffle jump
  - Block reach
  - Crossover shuffle
  - Going to a shuffle / crossover and someone standing as a guard

# 3 vs 3 (Half courts)
- Hitter, setter, defender
  - Every 6 points swap defenders
  - Hitter and setter block
    - Call evverything yo usee

# Forming the block
- Footwork and joining it all together
  - Steps and footwork (using what we did in the warmup)

# Playing 6 points out, calling everything you see as blockers on the serving side
- Looking at what the blockers are watching

# Playing 22-19
- Rotate on sideout
- Seeing block getting behind the ball
- Defense is low

# Game 
First to 25
