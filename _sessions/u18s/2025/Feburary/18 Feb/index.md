# Warmup
- Stretches, plank and push ups with each other
- Leg raises and partner is pushing against legs
- Pepper

# Triad Passing (3 person pepper without the net)
- Teams of 3 (player in middle and 2 to sides)
- Start with a set and a throw, into a hit
- Target to move each time

Working on passing angles and arm contact

# Over the net
- No jump, control on the pass
- Call to action (name and action)
  - Rotate the setter, hitting behind 3m line into swing

Working on high arm, shuffle into swing and talking throughout

# 4 on court 2 off 
- Rotate each time
- Back court hitting only
  - Name and action

Passing control and communication

# Defensive butterfly 
- Posture arms to target
  - Platform and movement
- Over the net throw to start

# Game
First to 25
