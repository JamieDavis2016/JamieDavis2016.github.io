# Warmup
- Pepper

# Skill 1: Approach;
- 3 step / 4 step approach
- Using that approach to catch ball at maximum height
- Getting left arm over right shoulder and reaching for the ball

# Skill 2: Hand Contact;
Kneeling down and hitting a fed ball under the net, using
- Thumb up
- Thumb down
- Wrist away

Then standing and shuffling into the fed ball for the hit

Lastly, Hitting approaching hitting at positions 1 and 5 using these techniques

# Skill 3: Defence
- Kneel down, work on single platform
  - Stand up, chest push to sides of defender
    - Keeping arms away from body
  - Trying with a hit / down ball from partner

Lastly, 15 up (same side defence)
