# Warmup (20mins)
- Ball control drill 30 reps before moving onto the next one and if the ball drops restart from the start 
- 4 minutes to complete all of them
   - Starting with volley and only 
   - Forearm only
   - Jump set
   - 2 contact and forearms pass
- Ball throws over the net (working on attacking technique)

# 4 Person pepper (half courts)
- We're playing over the net, staying static in positions, just going for 3 touches and an attacks.
  - Add a rotation through
    - Every 3rd touch at least a 2 step approach, jump and hit, if this doesn't happen, we rotate off

# Setter at 1
- Playing a regular game with the setter being at position 1, everyone sets and rotates to be a setter

# Game
Extra rules, every 3rd touch front court we want a swing, regardless of outcome
if there is no swing, you're off the court for a point (this means there would be 5 on court for the opposition to attack)
First to 25
