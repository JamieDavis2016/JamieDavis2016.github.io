# Warmup (20mins)
- Ball control drill 30 reps before moving onto the next one and if the ball drops restart from the start 
- 4 minutes to complete all of them
   - Starting with volley and only 
   - Forearm only
   - Jump set
   - 2 contact and forearms pass
- Ball throws over the net (working on attacking technique)

# Approach work
Looking at 2 / 3 step approaches, more experienced hitters hitting at 1 or 5
- Adding ina block and a defender
- U16's to go through approaches

# Self toss
- We're using a self toss from 4 to hit at position 5 (looking at aim)
- Play rally out if we miss on the attack, a free ball is ready to go in
- Choose between jump and no jump
  - If no jump staying off the net

# Game
Extra rules, 2 points for a deep hit
First to 25
