# Warmup (20mins)
- Ball control drill 30 reps before moving onto the next one and if the ball drops restart from the start 
- 4 minutes to complete all of them
   - Starting with volley and only 
   - Forearm only
   - Jump set
   - 2 contact and forearms pass

# Technical
- Using a tennis ball, we snap through and use our hips, throwing towards partners learning to torque and keep our body upright
- Using the net and snapping cross court

Then going into our 2 step approach body low and ready
- On one side of the court, using antenna and attacking the ball
- Other side is a toss with cones guiding the footwork and attack

Additionally using a horse (small thing to stand on and use), to teach players to torque

# Game
Extra rules, every 3rd touch front court we want a swing, regardless of outcome
if there is no swing, you're off the court for a point (this means there would be 5 on court for the opposition to attack)
First to 25
