# Warmup (30 mins)
Introduction of R.A.M.P (Raise, Activate, Mobilize, Potentiate)

- Plyos / conditioning
  - Feet, legs, back, core, shoulders
  - Leg raises, shoulder taps, back stretches
- Ball over defence (R)
- Core holds (A)
- Ball throws at net (M)

# Butterfly with movement
- Looking at a simple butterfly with service just to get warm and using all movement techniques
- Introduce a set and attack (catch for attacker)

# Attacking tempo (4 step approach)
- Practice our 4 step approach
- Watching tthe ball on last 2 and catching up high 
  - Look at a GO and RIP (12 / 32)

Adding the block into the attack
- Out of system set for middles every 3 balls (running a 51 or opening up to a 32)
- After 8 good quality hits
- Defenders in to play the hit out

# Meanwhile for the libero
