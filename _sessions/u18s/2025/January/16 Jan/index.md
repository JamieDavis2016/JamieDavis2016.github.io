# Warmup (15 mins)
- Pepper, working on defence and a serve like ball
- 3m, 2lines, serve, defend, rotate

# Service and serve reception 
- 2 Defenders, half courts
- Working on 3 steps to the ball footwork pattern

Rotate every 8 serves
* If you're feeling brave you can work on your jump floats as well

# Full court serve reception
- Servers, looking to serve cross or line
- 3 Defenders
Swapping every 8 passes

# Game
First to 25
Introducing the tempo sets and calls we want
