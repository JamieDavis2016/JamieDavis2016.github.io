# Warmup (15 mins)
- Volleytennis
  - Singles
  - Doubles

## Defensive sweet spot;
Looking at above the hand and below the elbow.

We're going to use Triangle passing for this
  - 1 player in the middle to set, 2 out to the sides and pass is to get to the moving setter / player in middle

# Butterfly drill 
- 1 to throw, throw to pass, pass to target, 3 minutes of this to build up

## Competitive version
- Choose to hit, if net have to throw (throwing overarm)
  - Passer is out if do not get a pass to target

# Square drill
- 2 vs 2, 2 front, 2 back
  - Set must come from back court and they pass forward, keeping simplicity

# Game
First to 25
Introducing the tempo sets and calls we want

# Notes;
- Freezing to target
- Droping shoulders
- Arm straight and then to target
- Face where the ball is coming from
