# Focus;
Looking at a 6:2 rotation, start with rotational side of things, then into in game rally

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 10 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
- Servers vs passers (to keep up with all the serve and SR work we've been doing)
- 3 vs 3 half court

# Going over 6:2 rotation
Printed 6:2 rotation slips for participants
- Playing a 6:2 rotation a few times

# Endless drill
- Whenever the ball drops, we go back to just before the mistake and repeat
I.E if an attack hits the net, we go back to the set, and the attacker goes again
