# Focus;
Transitions off the net to become ready for our attack, looking at getting to our 2 / 3 steps, this should happen all the time

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 13 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup (half court games)
- 2's ball over transition off to play for attack, 
  - add jump later on and full length (playing 3m to start)
- 3's ball in
  - playing with tempo with middle and pipe

# Footwork transition practice
- Coming off the net and into our approach (helping a few individuals)

Whilst doing this everyone is doing hitting lines 

# Hitting lines 
- 2 feeders, 2 setters, 2 passers, 3 hitters
  - 3 minute cycles
- Starting on the net, getting off the net
- L, R, L footwork

# Game 
First to 25
