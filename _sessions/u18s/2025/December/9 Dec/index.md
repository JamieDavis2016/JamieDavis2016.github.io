We had a rough tournament so switched up our serve and reception programme for a break

# Focus;
Working on our transition from offence to defence being able to see the ball at all times

# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup (half courts)
- 2 vs 2, 3 vs 3
  - Volley only on attack, getting behind the ball
  - Then into a standing swing
  - Then a jump attack

## Breaking down our approach
Just a few minutes going through our attacking approach and what our legs should be trying to do

# Hitting lines 
- 2 feeders, 2 setters, 2 passers, 3 hitters
  - 3 minute cycles
- Starting on the net, getting off the net
- L, R, L footwork
- added in defenders

# Game 
First to 25
