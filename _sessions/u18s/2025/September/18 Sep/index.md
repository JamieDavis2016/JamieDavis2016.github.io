# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup 
1, 2, 3 touch, 3 vs 3 (going from 1 touch volleyball, to 2 touches, to 3 touches)

# Serving and SR practice
- Serving between 2 passers, target is the setter and adding in an attack that catches the ball to reset

# Defensive system
Going through Perimeter defence (using a whiteboard)

We then play a ball in from outside (left front), middle (middle front) and then opposite (right front), looking at how everyone adjusts based on those attacks
