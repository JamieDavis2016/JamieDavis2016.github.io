# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Focus;
Getting to the ball with your legs first, using shuffle steps 80% of balls to be hittable

# Warmup
- Defensive dropback, legs wider than shoulders
  - hard driven attack, into a tip ball (groups of 3 in a triangle for target)
  - 6 - 8 reps rotate

  ## Setting work
  Setters, on the other side of the court, using a bounced ball in, turning body out and running getting behind the ball into a left, right, left set push (squaring up)

# Using the high pass for an attack
- 2 half courts, 2 passers, 2 attackers, 1 setter, 1 feeder
  - High ball pass, set to middle / pipe
  - 6 - 8 reps rotate around

# Square drill
- 2 back, 2 front, (then on other side) 2 front, 2 back
  - rotate going 2 back -> 2 front, 2 front -> 2 front, 2 front -> 2 back
- Fed ball in (not served), aiming for back court to take the 2 passes and 2 attackers to attack

# 3 Back court
- 2 rotate at 1 / 5, 6 goes to 1 / 5

# Game 
First to 25
