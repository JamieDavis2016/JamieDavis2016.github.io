# Resources
- 12 players
- 20+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup 
- Ball control; 3m to end line
  - Self toss and swing, defensive stance and try for a pass
- Making it a continious pass and attack
- Pepper, pass into hit

# Over the net pepper 2 vs 2 (ladders)
- Defenders pass up and again for the attacker (2 passes on the defender)
- First to 5
- Winner goes up the ladder, defeated goes down

# Double take (credit to Volleyball Fundamentals book)
- Ball in from coach
  - 1st ball up, 2nd ball to attacker
  - Bump passing, HIGH for attacker

# Calling SR on setter
- Wash into in rally play
  - into free ball

# Game - Setter in 1
First to 25
