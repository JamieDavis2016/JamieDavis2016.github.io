# Resources
- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 12 players
- 6+ balls, 1 court

# Warmup
- Pepper going side to side, of court
  - Double height of antenna
  - Legs and arms straight
  - 2 touches into 1

## Brazilian Volleytennis
- 2 balls, 2 players
  - Forearms only (into anything goes)
  - Must score both points to win a point, first to 10

# Square drill
- 2 back, 2 front, (then on other side) 2 front, 2 back
  - rotate going 2 back -> 2 front, 2 front -> 2 front, 2 front -> 2 back
- Fed ball in (not served), aiming for back court to take the 2 passes and 2 attackers to attack
- Emphasis on back court between 1 and 5 to take the ball
- Aiming for a hit on both sides before the ball drops
  - By hit, an approach and swing

# Service and serve receive
- left, right seams
- platform to ball in 3 steps

# Serving hoops
- A little experimental drill
  - using hoops on court, servers have to get it into the hoop, then go stand in hoop they can catch
- A race between the two teams and have to get everyone into the hoops first

# Game 
- Overpass, opposition gets a point
- if an attack doesn't happen, no point on sideout
- setter to give every front row attacker a swing before back court
First to 25
