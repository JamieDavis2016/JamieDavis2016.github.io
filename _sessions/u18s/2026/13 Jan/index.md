## Here we are going to work on our approach and using a bit of tempo in our attacks

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3 half court
Service onto the pass, pass to the setter and we're going to work on a tempo attack
  - The middle is going to be on their last 2 steps as the setter touches the ball
  - The outside is going to start their approach as the middle jumps
  - Try to play the rally out after the attack

We'll do 8 reps, swapping outside and middle each time before swapping over, everyone is going to have a go at setting as well as the attacks

- We're then going to change this to be the middle (middle front) and pipe (middle back)

# Activity 3 - Cross court pepper
- Using the tempo system we just learned

A live serve, pass into the setter, we're going to try and run the tempo for the middle and outside, playing the rally out the rest of the time
IF serve is not in, free ball to initiate the play

4 is going to attack what is called a rip or 32 (the outside attacker comes into the court more)

  - Positions 2, 3, 4, 5 are in court, 1 and 6 are out
  - We're going to takeaway 4 and put in position 1
  meaning that positions 2,3,5,6 are in court, 1 and 4 are out

(still working on the tempo of each other)
