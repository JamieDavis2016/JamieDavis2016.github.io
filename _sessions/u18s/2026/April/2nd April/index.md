# Choosing space on the court

# resources
- 8+ players
- 6+ balls
- 1hour 30 mins

# Focus 
Choosing space on the court that is open and attacking there

# Activity 1 - Touch and go
Playing within the 3m line, everytime the ball is touched, touch the endline, cannot touch the ball before going to the endline, play is as normal outside of the 3m line and running to endline

- Start with a serve in from behind the 3m line

Play to points of 15 (the losing side has a punishment that the winning side can pick (within reason...)) 
- 3 points if there's calls about where's open
- 2 points into space where there is no one

# Activity 2 - Serving and SR
3 servers 3 passers (1 attacker) and a setter on one side
- 2 that are not serving can play on court (either as a blocker or defender) 
- Can choose to return the ball or stop there

Rotating passers into the rotation and having an attack back to the other side of the court, the attacker can choose where they attack from (2, 3 or 4)

Servers to play to 8 points, receive to play to 10 points (to win a point on receive an attack must happen)

(the losing side has a punishment that the winning side can pick (within reason...))

We can also attempt to look at some jump floats for individuals (if interested)

# Activity 3 - Cross court pepper
4 vs 4 pepper, starting with players at positions 2, 4, 5 and 6

Play to 7 points
This is a wash drill, so service into a free ball, rotation we can choose to be on when the ball goes over, when it drops and setters can choose if they want to stay or rotate

Different areas of the court are out of bounds (I.E position 1 is out of bounds when we play at 5 and 6)

## Cooperative version
Trying to get 15 touches before the ball drops, whilst still attempting an attack at where's in court

## Competitive version
Playing to win both small points first

## Fun version (can be attempted at the end)
2 touches from the same person and play the ball over

Get a big point IF the attack lands with no one to defend within 1 - 2 meters (regardless of 2nd ball)
