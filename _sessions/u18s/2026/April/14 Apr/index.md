# Creating setter and attacker variation
- Players - 17

- Balls - 6+

- 2 courts 

- 1 hour 30

# Focus
Creating better setter variation for our attackers (adding in a back set)

# Warmup

## U18s
2 vs 2
- The attacker has to decide to go in front or behind, 2 points if the attack comes from behind the setter
- The pass is to aim for the net / front court

Can start with no jump on the attack to warmup

Then add a jump in when we fill warm and a block

### Next variation
The set has to now go back court, for the attacker

## U16s
- 2 vs 2 half courts (+ 1 that's a 3), 2 points if the attack comes from behind the setter
- Anywhere on court

Can start with no jump on the attack to warmup

Then add a jump in when we fill warm and a block

### Next variation
The set has to now go back court, for the attacker

# Activity 2 

## U18s
3 vs 5, play the rally out

- The 5 side is serving, 3 front and 2 back court
- 3 side is receiving, can choose to have a setter or play anywhere
  - 5 has 1 spot out of bounds
  - 3 has to get an attack to win the point

If either side has a back set and an attack with it, they score 2 points

## U16s
3's rotating in, queen of the court style
- Setter starts at position 1, with our serve receive taking up the whole court
- The court is past the half way line for in rally

The player next to the setter can attack from the middle or behind the setter for 3 points

# Activity 3 - Serving

## U16s
- Target service (aiming at spots on the court)

## U18s
- Trying a jump float service

# Activity 4 - Cross Court play

## U18s 
Positions are going to be - 1, 2, 5 and 4 or 6
  - If you choose 4 your setter is back court at position 1, if you choose 6 your setter is front court at position 2

Starting cooperatively with trying to get to 15 touches (can be volleys over to start)
Once at 15 becomes a downball / volley
Once at 15 again becomes a jump and attack

## U16s
Positions are going to be - 1, 2, 5 and 4 or 6
  - If you choose 4 your setter is back court at position 1, if you choose 6 your setter is front court at position 2

Starting cooperatively with trying to get to 15 touches (can be volleys over to start)
Once at 15 becomes a downball / volley
Once at 15 again becomes a jump and attack

# Game
If a back set happens with an attack, you score a point mid rally
