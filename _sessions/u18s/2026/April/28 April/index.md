# Shot selection, creating how we can use our shots

# resources
- 7 / 8 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 / 2 coaches
- 1hour 30 mins
- 1 court

# Focus
Creating an emphasis on our shot selection, how we can use our shots to score points (sub focus; look at where the opposition is not)

# Warmup
- Playing 3 vs 3 / 4 vs 4 whilst everyone enters, no jump on our attack, tracking and attacking deeper into the court

# Activity 1 - Left hand push
- Playing a 2 vs 2 half court game, with a serve in

Adding in a 50% attack for power, looking at a controlled attack, the set is aiming to be lower than the top of the antenna, for height
looking at using our guiding arm, using that to attack the ball over our right shoulder, and creating a consistent right, left attack approach

On the defending side, 1 blocker and 1 defender off at the attack line

## 1.1
Looking at a high ball at the net
The defender goes behind the attack line, being deeper into the court

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 4 or 4 vs 4, fast ball, set
- Positions 2, 4, 5 and 6 are in the court (position 1 is out)

The ball has to be lower than the antenna to score a point, with an attack, what does this change about our attack? can we still hit deep into the court

- If you place it between the floor pads you get 3 points


# Activity 3 - 3 vs 4 or 4 vs 4, high ball, set
- Positions 2, 4, 5 and 6 are in the court (position 1 is out)

The ball has to be higher than the antenna to score a point, with an attack, what does this change about our attack? can we still hit deep into the court

- If you place it between the floor pads you get 3 points

# Game
- 4 vs 4, positions 1, 2, 4 and 5 are in bounds (6 is out of bounds)
