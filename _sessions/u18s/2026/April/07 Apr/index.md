# Deep parts of the court

# resources
- 12+ players
- 6+ balls
- 1hour 30 mins
- 2 courts

# Focus 
Using the deep parts of the court when and looking when they're open 50% of the time

A arm swing technique video

# Activity 1 - Dodgeball catch and throw
Starting off with 2 vs 2 half courts 
- If a defender touches the ball they win the point
- Catching and throwing, only the end 3m of the court is open
- Can use your approach or standing
- Can block as well 

# 1.1 - 3 vs 3 full court
- Opening up the whole court and now deciding where to shoot

Pairing up with another player (you're buddies so picking an U18 with an U16)

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 4, 2 vs 2
- Defenders inside the 3m line
- Live serve in (no penalty on any serve you choose)
- Attacker can hit anywhere
Team must attempt deep on at least 1 of every 2 attacks

- Setter also starts at position 1 and has to run forward (from service)

# 2.2 
With the 3 defenders, do we want to add a block or keep 3 back? (talking about what's open on the court)

# Activity 3 - Closing the court
- First 10 points, position 1 (RB) is not part of the court 
- Next 10 points, position 5 (LB) is not part of the court
- Last 10 points, position 6 (MB) is not part of the court

# Activity 4 - 4 vs 2
Playing to 7 points, 4 attackers, 2 defenders
- 4 attackers side, setter is at 1 and coming from right back into the court
- Attackers can only hit deep into the court 
- Live serve from defenders
- If defenders get a touch, they win the point (except for service)
Team must attempt deep on at least 1 of every 2 attacks

# Game
- Deep court kill = 2 points 
- Some extra rules for certain players
Team must attempt deep on at least 1 of every 2 attacks
