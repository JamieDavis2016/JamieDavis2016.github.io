# How we block and playing with the intent to block as well as creating an attack
- Players - 18
9 U18s 
10 u16s

- Balls - 6+

- 2 courts 

- 1 hour 30

# Focus
Creating an attack opportunity that's fast around the blockers, what are we watching as blockers? And where are we attacking as attackers?
Trying to get an attack  50% of the time

# Warmup - Half court games

## U18s
2 groups of 3 and 2 groups of 2

Then we are going to add an attack in where you can only score a point if the attack is from front court

On the serve receive side, your setter will start at 1 for the 3 side, 1 side is always serve receive and 1 is always serve, the 2 side is the serving side and the 3 side is serve receive, every 5 points someone swaps from the 3 side to the 2 side

On the serving side, we're going to have 1 blocker and 1 in defense. What does the blocker look for? What are we trying to block and why do we block?

## U16s
2 groups of 3 and 2 groups of 2

Half court game

Then we are going to add an attack in where you can score 2 more points if the set is in the attack zone

On the serve receive side, your setter can start at position 1 or 2 for the 3 side, 1 side is always serve receive and 1 is always serve, the 2 side is the serving side and the 3 side is serve receive, every 5 points someone swaps from the 3 side to the 2 side

On the serving side, we're going to have 1 blocker and 1 in defence. What does the blocker look for? What are we trying to block and why do we block?


# Activity 1 - Small court with a block

## U18s - 3 vs 3, small width court
3 vs 3, small width court (about 3 arms lengths of the court)

Playing a serve in, with the serving side, there are 2 blockers (which may take up a big portion of the court)

queen of the court style games

How can we play around a block and a court this size? Can we attack fast? Can we attack around or even over the blockers?

# U16s - Half court 3 vs 3
3 vs 3 queen of the court style

With 1 player blocking (doesn't have to jump but hands up), a zone at the back of the court worth 3 points for the attackers 

# Activity 2 -

## U18s 3/4ths of the court
5 vs 5

3 front and 2 back,

With a smaller portion of the court, we should look at how we get 2 or 3 blockers to the ball, or why we may have a blocker drop off

Starting with a serve with a wash into a free ball (that can be a tip or a deep ball so looking at where)

# U16s - Half court 3 vs 3
We now are going to have 2 blockers and only a small part of the court to cover, trying to look at how we can play off the block with an attack OR get around the block

Trying hitting into the block? Or playing around or through the block!

# Activity 3 - Full court games

# U18s 
5 vs 5 or 6 vs 4

If we go 5 vs 5, 3 blockers at the front, can score an extra point if the attack only has a single block

or 6 vs 4, with a look at rotation

If we are the 6 side we are serving, blockers are watching what we do and defense ready to play the ball

The 4 get a point on a kill
The 6 get a point if they jump with the hitter, and tracking the ball after the jump

# U16s
- 5 vs 5

With 3 blockers at the front on the serving side 

And 3 in the serve receive on the receiving side, a setter at 1

Now we with our blockers and the defense. How can we 1. Defend? 2. Get an attack back over?

# Game
Regular play

## U18s
If we get 2 blockers against an attack with each other, gain an additional point

## U16s
If we get 1 blocker against an attack, gain an additional point
