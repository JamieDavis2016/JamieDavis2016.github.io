## S&C
- Arm throws
- 3 step approach, alternating (working on right handed approach and left)
- Squat jumps for the losing team

# Warmup; 3 vs 3 half courts
 - dig, set, volley
 - volley, volley, volley
 - dig, set, standing attack
 - 2 Front court 1 back court

# Warmup 2; 2 vs 2 half courts
We setup a designated hitter, from serve receive

The other player is a passer

Having a thrown ball in (or challenge with a serve)

The attacker takes the first ball and sets up to attack the 3rd touch
The 2nd touch is now the setter / passer

Playing a Bo5 with the setter and attacker changing when we rotate


- Cues; Getting hip to ball on your last step AND aiming to be on our first left step of a L-R-L when setter touches the ball

# Activity 1; 4 vs 4
Serve receive unit starting just in front of the 3m line (attack line)
- Serve in, then a free ball, with attacker at the net 
(how do we adjust as an attacker here?)

Rotating QOC style
- We have left side, middle and pipe hitter

Zone 6 is out of bounds

Cues; Staying available as a hitter
When setter is off the net, what do we look to do?

# Activity 1B; 4 vs 4
- Setter is now back court

Rotating QOC style
- We have left side, middle and right side hitter

Zones 6 is out of bounds

Cues; When setter is back court, how do we all stay ready as hitters?

# Activity 2; 6 vs 6
- Setter is at position 1

Everyone rotates to play as the setter when at position 1, you can play anywhere you want front court.

# Gameplay
We're still looking at how to convert with an attack

So the scoring is getting an extra point if we attack from serve receive
(meaning we can score 2 points, 1 for attacking and 1 for wining the rally)
