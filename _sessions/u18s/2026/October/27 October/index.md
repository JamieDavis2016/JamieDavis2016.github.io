# Consistency in attacks

# Focus; Hand contact and looking at how we control our tempo and swing based of a L-R-L approach (Or R-L approach). Can we consistently attack the ball

# Resources
- 9+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

## videos

# Warmup; Establishing good hand contact
In pairs, we're going to do a pepper like drill, looking at hand contact

Can we create the ball to spin, to our target

Partner is going to pass it up and try to attack in 2 (if that doesn't happen play for 3 touches and try to get the attack back over)


# Activity 1; Slow and fast tempo
A setter, 2 attackers and a passer on one side

A feeder and someone to help feed, 3 in defence

Working on our L-R-L footwork with our attack and hand contact

Going to go for a few minutes the attackers swap each attack

Working with a tempo like system off of the setter, the middle should be jumping as the setter touches the ball

The outside should be starting their approach as the middle jumps
