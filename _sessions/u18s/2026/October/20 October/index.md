# Warmup; Establishing good hand contact
In pairs, we're going to do a pepper like drill, looking at hand contact

Can we create the ball to spin, to our target

Partner is going to pass it up and try to attack in 2 (if that doesn't happen play for 3 touches and try to get the attack back over)

# Activity 1; 1 vs 1 over the net (1/3rd of the court)
We have a setter on both sides, with an attacker every 5 points we rotate the attacker and setter

A free ball thrown over and we're after creating that hand contact again, the back 3ms of the court are in

# Activity 2; 3 vs 3 half courts
2 front court and 1 back court

A service in (as we still want to practice our serve receive)

2 front court players are committing to the block and the defender has to look at the open space behind the block

As an attacker we now pick to attack into the block, around the block, working with that arm / hand connection

## Note; A quick run through of perimeter defence
What our roles are and why I.E our outside plays back middle and our middles play back left, rightside / setter playing right back


# Activity 3; 6 vs 5
Playing to points of 7

The 5 has 3 on court, a defender at 5, an attacker at 4 and a setter

The 6 has our normal defensive system

When we have played to points of 7 we'll introduce the middle and opposite roles

# Ending drill
Hitting from positions 4, 3 and 2

With a defender and a free ball coming in

We have to hit the ball hard otherwise it doesn't count, the ball has to go in court and we're after a count of 15
