# What does our block do?
- Players - 10

- Balls - 6+

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30


# Focus 
Back court cover, with the blocker, looking at where's open and defending the ball if we can get 20% in rally defence that's a good start

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play / 3 vs 3
Warming up aiming for attacks to be hitting in the last 3m of the court

Start with no jump

Once jumps is added in, wanting the ball to the net as a front court attack (hitting deep)

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 6
- 4 side is receiving with 3 being front court

6 side is serving, the 6 side has no blockers to start, we'll play to 5 points before rotating
and swapping a new set of players, the winners can decide if they move or stay
- a wash drill so service, free ball / down ball to 4 side and then a free ball / downball to 6 side

What does that mean to our defence? What happens to our base positions?


## Activity 2.1 - Adding in 1 blocker
What happens to our defence when we add in a middle blocker?

## Activity 2.2 - Adding in 2 blockers
What happens to our defence when we add in 2 wing blockers and no middle blocker?

## Activity 2.3 - Adding in 3 blockers
What happens to our defence when we add in 2 wing blockers and a middle blocker?

How should our defence adapt to this? What are we looking for?

# Activity 3 - 3 before 7
Serving side has 4 players

Receiving side has 6 players

The serving side has to defend 3 balls before the receiving side scores 7 points

# Game
If we get a defensive pass to the setter you get an extra point

Scoring a kill between 1 and 6 or  5 and 6 will give you 2 points
