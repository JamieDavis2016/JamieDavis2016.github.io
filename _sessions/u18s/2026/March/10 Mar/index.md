# Back court coverage (mainly positions 5 and 6)
- Players - 9

- Balls - 10+

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30


# Focus 
Looking at 5 and 6 back court coverage, what are our roles? Can we get a pass even if it's 20% of the time

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play / 3 vs 3
Warming up aiming for attacks to be hitting in the last 3m of the court

Start with no jump

Once jumps is added in, wanting the ball to the net as a front court attack (hitting deep)

# Activity 2 - Hitting lines 
A setter at right front (2), 2 attackers left front and middle and a passer at 5

5 in defence on the other side, looking to defend against the 2 attackers

# Activity 3 - 3 before 7
Serving side has 4 players, middle front, left front, right back and middle back

Receiving side has 5 players, right back, left back, middle back, left front and right front (when we go to in rally, your setter is left back to start)

The attack cannot hit at positions 1 and 3 (tip into middle)

The serving side has to defend 3 balls before the receiving side scores 7 points

We add a downball in after service ball is done with

# Game
Scoring a ball between 5 and 6 will give you 2 points
Defending a ball between 5 and 6 will give you 3 points
