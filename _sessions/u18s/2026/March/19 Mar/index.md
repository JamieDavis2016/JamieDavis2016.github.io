# Out of system play
- Players - 8

- Balls - 6+

- 1 court 

- 1 hour 30


# Focus 
Out of system play, aiming to have an attack 50% of the time, hitter to stay open to the ball allowing them to have attacking options

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play
- Live serve

3 contacts with an attack (hopefully at the net)

When the ball goes over, touch the 3m line and go into defence (can choose 1 at the net and 1 off or 2 off, or even 2 block!)

# Activity 2 - Double touch pass into an attack
- Ball served in to 4 side (lets say 3 front 1 back)

Passer, passes to self then to attacker, or straight to attacker (front row)

When served ball is done, a free ball is put into the winning side

Have to win both to win a point, we'll play to 7

# Activity 3 - Same as activity 2
But now have 2 back row (right back and middle back) and 2 front row (left front) (Middle)

- A pass to self and then pass out to attacker

# Game
If we have an out of system play (pass is not to 3m line at right front (2)) with an attack then you win 3 points
