# Warmup
3 vs 3, half court
- Start with no jump (add jump later)
- Serve initiation from half court in, working on float contact

With each other call scores on the pass
Pass inside setter zone = 2
Pass outside zone but playable = 1
Shanked = 0

Calling for the ball (mine or go) before it reaches the net, getting into position to pass

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 2 servers vs passers
- Both sides of court
3 defenders / receives, 1 setter
the player at 5 is an outside (4) hitter, swap between hitter and setter
2 servers

## Earning the right to serve
We only serve if your side gets to side-out with an attack

We go to 15 points on serve receive before rotating,
3 pass (score of pass) + in-system attack attempt = 3
2 pass (score of pass) + attack attempt = 2
1 pass (score of pass) + attack attempt = 1

## Change where the hitter is

Move from the attacker being at 4 to being at 2 (right side / opposite) or 3 (middle front)

Or we can add that when the serve receive has happened, you have more attacking options

# Activity 3 - In system attack
- Must play one in system attack to win the point

# Game
- We cannot score unless there is an in system attack
- Going to keep track of our sideout, from a pass to the attack (we are aiming for 50+%)

If an attack happens off of an in system attack, it counts as 2
If an attack happens off of an out of system attack, it counts as 1
Otherwise 0

We are going to calculate the sideout score with this:
Total serve receive opportunities
Total in-system attack attempts

Then calculate:
In-system attack % =
(in-system attack attempts ÷ total serve receives) × 100
