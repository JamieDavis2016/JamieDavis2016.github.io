# Attacking consistency, hitting

# resources
- 8+ players
- 8+ balls
- 1hour 30 mins

(This is after a tournament on the Sunday before)

# Focus
Attacking consistency, hitting deeper court shots, understanding what happens on each attack, I.E footwork, arm swing, timing etc.
We want 50% of the balls hit to be deeper into the court at 6 or 1

# Warmup
- General play without a jump just to build back into the session

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play OR touch and go
- We have a choice between these 2.

## 2 vs 2
- We have a no jump to start, playing off the net and attacking the ball, swapping each time the ball goes over
- Service initiation (not back full length just yet)

Once warm, jump is allowed and back court attacks
- The next part is the initiation will change from a serve

Player at the net, can choose to downball, push or overhead throw, passer must pass to player at net, set back and go into an attack from that

## Touch and go
- Using the full width, but 3m line running to end line after touch and communicating what's happening such as I'm out the pass, or where's open on the court, going cross or short
- Other rule is you cannot go back to the same spot

# Activity 2 - Cross Court Variations
- Players at 2, 4, 6 and 2 servers ready (rotate when the ball ends)
Hitting into 6


No jump on attack to start, Setter at 2 is to put the ball to the 3m line

Adding in the jump and footwork as we go through


- Changing variation to be attacking at position 1,
This means we may have players at 1, 2, 4 and 6 (or 1, 2, 4)

# Activity 3 - Service
Only a few minutes to dust off the service
- Working on general services
  - Adding in targeted serves at 1 and 5


# Activity 4 - Back row 4's (or full game)
If we cannot make the numbers, we'll go into having a setter at 2 and playing every hitter is back row and can only attack from behind the 3m line

Still working on an attack approach and swing


## Note;
Taking the time to work with a few players on smaller technical pieces as these drills go on.
