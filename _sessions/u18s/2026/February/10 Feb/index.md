# Warmup
- 2 vs 2 half court, pass, set, hit
  - No jump
  - Ball initiation is going to be from the net to your partner, as an attack or an overhead push
- Play points and rotate players in

Playing the pass and attacking over the net without a jump to start

Once more warm we add a jump in

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 full court
- Attack only scores if past the attack line (3m), attacking deep into court (a bit cooperatively)
- Rotate through under the net and round
- Players initiate ball in with a throw or downball (whichever is comfortable)
- Trying to play 3 touches with an attack on the 3rd touch


Between you and partner, discuss if you want a front court or back court attack

Must be an attack before rotation, if it doesn't happen we just go again (3 tries before rotating)

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3, player at 4
Continuing on this theme, we're now adding in a player at the front (4) position
- May swap the player to be at 1 as well

Continuing to rotate, we want to have an attack is the only way to score the point, the front court can hit front, the other positions can only hit back court

Adding in noodles as blockers, to play around in defence

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4
- 2 front and 2 back

Setter starts back row, when ball is played to either we want the other to take the 2nd and get an attack to either wing player

For now lets keep it simple and pass the way you're facing, we will remove later

# Activity 4 - 5 vs 5
- Setter front and back court swap still
- 2 front and 3 back

We now want to keep the same idea and rotate players around like a normal game after every ball drop

# Activity 5 - Defensive positioning
- 6 on 1 side, 4 / 5 on the other
Setter initiates a throw, the 4 side are on a bench to imitate different places to the ball will be attacked

Defensive move to the positions based on where the attack comes from and reset.

# Game
Play to 25,
Scoring a point each time the ball is hit with at least a 2 step approach
