# Warmup - Touch and go
- Playing full width, within the 3m line
- Everytime the ball is touched, run to end line and playing somewhere else
Looking to introduce communication, discussing a calls to make with each other (I'm a fan of mine and go)

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 server passer (half courts)
- Serving on 1 side and receiving to play the rally out on the other
Both players are off the net receiving with each other

Do we want to mix up every 5 points?

Looking at an attack on 2nd or 3rd contact
- 2 points for an attack point
- 1 point for a free ball

Again after a call with each other, mine / go maybe?

# Activity 2 - Service on one side into rally
- 5 on the receiving side, 3 defenders an attacker and possible setter
  Aiming to put an attack in off of service

- 2 / 3 players on court for the receiving side to play the rally out

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 4 / 5 vs 5
- If an attack happens on the serve receive side an additional point
  - Rotating round everyone playing everywhere

Looking to have those same calls to help each other with who to go for the ball
