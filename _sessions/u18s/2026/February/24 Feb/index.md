# Warmup - Serving and passing changes
Servers can serve short or deep and the passer has to read it from their position
- Serving from behind 3m line (can choose)
  - Looking at serving a float serve, technical cue, look for no spin on the ball with a solid hand

Passer is going to start inside the 3m line and drop back to pass the ball

## Part 2;

Service still from behind 3m line

Passers are going to start from near end line and run in to pass

# Activity 1 - Serving lines
Serving on one side (6 servers), 4 passers, 2 targets on the other

Serving at the line (as we have 2 passers, on both halfs of the court)
2 lines, at positions 1 and 5, focus on passing

## Activity 1 - Changing seams
Now going to 2 passers at positions 5 and 6 on both sides of the court, serving coming from position 1

Rotating passers out

# Activity 2 - 2 vs 2
Serving on one side 

Serve receive on the other

Both players in the pass and attempting to get a front row attack side out

# Activity 3 - Service and sideout
6 on serve receive side, 3 on the other side and 3 servers

Running a pass and offence to an attack, attempting to sideout 50% (an accurate pass to a front row attacker, to count for sideout)

# Game 
- If sideout gets an attack, you get a point in the rally

Looking at calculating sideout % on both sides (an accurate pass to a front row attacker, not a kill)

Looking at using these;
Example:

20 serves received
11 rallies won
11÷20=55%

OR

20 serves received
9 accurate passes to front row attacker
9÷20=45%
