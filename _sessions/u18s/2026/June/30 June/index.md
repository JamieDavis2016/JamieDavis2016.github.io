# Serve reception patterns to the ball

# resources
- 9 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Focus
Building serve and serve reception cues, being fast in serve receive fighting to get behind the ball 80% of the time, and for service building a faster service

# Warmup;
(starting without resistance bands)
-> Half courts (half width)
-> Servers on 1 side

-> Passers on the other

-> A target for the pass

-> Passers will have resistance bands added in, around their ankles, and look to explode to the ball

-> Server are going to look at, generating an overarm serve and how to contact the ball

-> Principles being slow to fast

(resistance bands going away after it)

(Cues:
-> Get behind the ball

-> Freeze platform to target

-> Be explosive (if we're going to our left, pushing off with our right leg)

-> Slow to fast
)
# Activity 1; Serve receive into offense
-> Building on from the warmup

-> 2 in the serve receive
-> A target
-> An attacker on the outside (or middle)

# Activity 2;
Now 3 players in the serve receive

A set of cones / floor mats around the general area of where players go in serve receive, they cannot go in between them (looking at other places on court)

Building an offensive attack, outside or middle attacks
