# How our attack interacts with defensive positioning


# Focus;
Working on creating our attack angles within the defensive system that we want to run, I.E hitting the cross or the line shot and how we actively defend against that

# Resources
- 10+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Getting our shoulders warm with some throws
### Purpose; warming up our shoulders

In pairs throwing dodgeballs (like we did last week) just for a few minutes to get our shoulders warm, working on rotating and using our hips to generate power

# Warmup 2; Getting our footwork approach down on a 51 and 31 ball
### Purpose; Getting our legs warm to hit with as well

We'll start with our players doing it in 3 lines and going round quickly

Then 2.1
I'll put a ball up in the air as the player is doing their approach, we're just going to start with catches, once warm we change it to adding in an attack

How do we get to a 31 for an attack and a 51 for our attack

# Activity 1: Hitting lines with a block
### Purpose; Developing our ability to attack into different areas of the court, particularly line and cross, using the approach and arm action from our warmup.

We'll have 2 hitting lines

2 setters, 1 setting the 4 ball and 1 setting the 2 ball

1 hitting line having 2 hitters, the other hitting line having 2 hitters and 1 lib

3 blockers and rotate every few minutes

Cues: Working on from our warmup finishing with our hitting arm high and a snap, looking at generating attacking areas such as line and cross shot

# Activity 2 - 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3
### Purpose; Live play with working on the attack

Letting live play happen after the hitting lines, looking at how we are finishing with our attack and where are our balls being set to

Cues; For setters high balls and fast release
For hitters, working on creating a tempo of slow to fast in our attack

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 6
### Purpose; Working on creating our hitting opportunities

This will have a live play element, serve and a down ball in

On the 4 side, 1 setter, 1 outside, 1 middle, 1 libero

Every 4 points we'll rotate players on the 4 side, 6 side rotates then as well

We'll get 2 points for hitting a line shot or a cross shot
