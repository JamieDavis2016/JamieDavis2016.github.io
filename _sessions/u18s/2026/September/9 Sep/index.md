# Looking at service and sideout stats

# Focus
Creating a sideout strategy and being aware of our sideout stats, ideally after 55%+ meaning we sideout more than the opposition serves

Sideout meaning: A side out in volleyball happens when the receiving team wins a rally, which stops the opponent's serve and earns the receiving team the right to serve next

# Resources
- 12+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup

## Warmup 1; Mechanics on how to generate power
### Purpose; Looking at how we generate a lot of power with our bodies, for serving and hitting 
Cues: Transfer of body weight, rotate hips / trunk and shoulder (torque) and lastly speed 

In pairs over the net, using a dodgeball and look at generating throws, we'll try some off stuff such as doing it on one leg, doing it with two and so on.


### Warmup 2, Serving game;
2 lines of servers

It's a race to get all the other servers to the other line first and back again!

Once a server gets the ball over and into the court, they can help get the ball back to their teammates

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half courts
## Purpose; We want to create lots of serve and serve receive. After creating an attack off of the serve receive
We are at a tug of war with each other, the server wants to stop a good pass and the serve receive is fighting to get a good pass

Setter is starting at positions 1 (so back court behind your receivers)

- Looking at creating a middle (medium tempo, set) and a pipe attack set for our 2 attackers (the setter can dump as well) and we can block
- Service cues; Transfer of body weight, rotate hips / trunk and shoulder (torque) and lastly speed
  - (challenge) If we can create that floatness of the serve as well and low to the net

- Our goal; creating an attack off of our serve receive

Games to points of 10 
- 1 for winning the rally
- 2 points for a sideout attack on serve receive

# Activity 2 - 6 vs 6
## Purpose; Creating our sideout structure and seeing what our stats tell us about it (we're after 55% and above!)
### Note / question; can we consistently turn serve receive into an organised attack?

- Every 3 serves calling a different rotation, with the rotation being called it's going to be where the setter should be

You have 10 seconds to figure it out before the ball gets played, if the positions are incorrect will give a point to the opposition

Playing to points of 11

Cues; What are we looking for as servers? Are we seeing gaps in the serve receive? What about where the setter is?

# Game
Watching rotational play and tracking our serve and serve receive stats for the game
