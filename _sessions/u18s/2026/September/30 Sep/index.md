# Warmup 1; 3 person pepper
### Purpose; Working on ball control and passing angles

In a 3 person pepper, 1 player is in the middle, they can move to anywhere

Another player is passing

Another player is attacking

The passer is trying to pass the ball to the player in the middle

# Activity 1: Balls in to pass, rotate 
### Purpose; Single person passing technique
In 2 lines working on our passing platform and aiming our serves, starting in and coming back to the ends of the court

The serving line is going to start with throws and progress to serving when we have a passing rate of around 80% 2 point passes

Some mats setup in the area to give you an idea of points within the ball pass

Cues; Holding your platform after the pass (more on the early platform, early angle)

# Activity 2: 2 person serving lines
### Purpose; Now having a setter after the pass, creating an attacking opportunity

Moving on from the 1 person serving line, we're now going to have a setter on 2 sides of the court

Including a defender that will now attack after the pass, the setter is setting the ball for the attacker

We're going to look at the pass to attack transition

Cues: Waiting for our attack after the pass, slow to fast

# Activity 3: Cross court pepper
### Purpose; We're looking at our serve receive portion to create an attack
With our 8 players we'll play a cross court pepper game to enable a full game

We'll cut off parts of the court to make it more around the game we're playing
(slight continuation on the half court game yesterday)

We're playing positions 6, 5, 4 (maybe 3) and 2

Your setter is at position 2 and we'll play a 3 person serve receive with the 4 player coming to the back left in serve receive hitting front court
