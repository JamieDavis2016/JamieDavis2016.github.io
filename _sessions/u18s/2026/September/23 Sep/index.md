# Building our serve receive unit part 1 (of 4)

We're going to come back to our serve receive unit 

# Focus;
Building our passing unit to enable an attack for the middle of the court, this should be achievable 50% of the time and the pass should be 1 / 2m off the net

# Resources
- 8+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Balls in to pass, rotate 
### Purpose; Single person passing technique
In 2 lines working on our passing platform and aiming our serves, starting in and coming back to the ends of the court

Cues; Early platform, early angle

# Warmup 2; 2 person passes, rotate
### Purpose; 2 person passing seams
Looking at passing 5 / 6 seam or 1 / 6 seam and working with a partner now

Cues; Looking at left short, right deep

# Activity 1: - 3 person serve receive
### Purpose; 3 person passing in our seams

Now adding in the 3rd person keeping our seam work in control

Serving on both sides, 2 times before swapping to the other side

The servers are trying to beat the passers by getting the ball out of system, 3 servers before 2 passes, when you get to 1 that's 1 big point

We'll play to 5 big points we swap passers and servers

Cues; early call, early platform and early angle

# Activity 2 - Building in a middle attack
### Purpose; With our passing system we're now implementing can we attack from the middle of the court with a 51 ball?

Adding in more serves with a middle attack, 4 attacks before swapping, keeping the same we played in activity 1

Cues; watching the middle and setter interaction from serve receive

Can we attack 50%? Going to take stats on this

# Activity 3 - Adding in an outside attack
### Purpose; We now have our outside and middle attack options

We now experiment with running an outside attack (from the player at position 5 now coming front court to attack)

We will have 2 blockers on the other side, and the setter is now making a decision on where to set for the attack, if the blockers close the gap they win the point
