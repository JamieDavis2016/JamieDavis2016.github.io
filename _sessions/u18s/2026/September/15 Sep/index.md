# Expanding on base defensive positions in perim defence


# Focus
Creating our perimeter defensive positioning based on where the attack is coming from, having an understanding of where to be and why. Getting to it at least 80% of the time

# Resources
- 12+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 1:30 hour
- 1 court

# Warmup 1; Defensive posture and timing on arms
### Purpose; We're going to look at how you handle defensive posture in the middle of a rally

In pairs we'll go through the technique quickly on the defensive stance to take and how to take it

## warmup 2; Defensive positioning based on the attackers
### Purpose; Seeing where we should position ourselves in rallies to handle a wing attacks

Into a 6 up start and we'll go through the positioning that's needed based on which attack and where it's coming from

# Activity 1 - 4 vs 4
### Purpose; Creating our defensive priority from serve receive
Queen of the court, serve receive and initial attack

We play the rally out in a 4 vs 4 style, everyone plays everywhere for this

Swapping blockers at the net, I.E 1 blocker and 2 blockers and seeing how that changes the defenders vision and choices

(Before Activity 2) - lets go through areas of concern in defence

# Activity 2 - 4 vs 6
### Purpose; Going through defence on each attacking position

This will have a live play element, serve and a down ball in

On the 4 side, 1 setter, 1 outside, 1 middle, 1 libero

Every 4 points we'll rotate players on the 4 side, 6 side rotates then as well

Cues; looking at defensive positioning based on where the attack is coming from
What do we see as defenders?
What do we see as blockers?
