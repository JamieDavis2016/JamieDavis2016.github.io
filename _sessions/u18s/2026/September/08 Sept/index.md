# Warmup
- 2 person, Brazillian Volleytennis with 2 balls

- 1.1 - Introducing a float serve, how to and what key parts there are to this

Cues; Palm to the ball, no fingers and torquing through the ball to generate power

# Activity 1 - Serving down the line
Using 2 lines on the end of the court,

3 passers, rotating round
3 servers on the other side

Cues; early platform, early angle

# Activity 1.1 - Cross court
Now looking at how we serve cross court

# Activity 2 - 3 person serve receive
Now with 3 in our serve receive and a setter rotating into positions 1, 5 and 4 (will provide where the setter should be and where they're going)
- 4 defenders on court total (x2 running across both sides of the net)

Scoring against each other, servers to get 6 points before defenders get 4 points

Swapping defenders and servers

Cues; Strong platform with calm arms (looking at how to not break platform like in one of the videos)

# Activity 3 - 3 vs 3 half courts
- Quick games of 3 vs 3 half court situations
- 1 front court and 2 back court (imagine setter being at 1 and coming in)

Working on creating an attack for the middle or pipe (back court attack)

Games to points of 10 

# Activity 4 - 6 vs 6
- Every 3 serves calling a different rotation, with the rotation being called it's going to be where the setter should be

You have 10 seconds to figure it out before the ball gets played, if the positions are incorrect will give a point to the opposition

Playing to points of 10

Cues; What are we seeing as defenders against the service? What can we call?

# Game
Every ball that's 2x the height of the antenna is a point
