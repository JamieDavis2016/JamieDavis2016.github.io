This is our first session back after the christmas break (desperately needed), we're looking at how we play our defence low

# Movement and getting low, how we do it

# Focus;
Getting back into movement and playing with defence, low and grounded. Looking at how we fall / dive safely

# Warmup
- 1 minute, each arm digs, 1 minute normal digs to self, 1 minute volleys to self and 1 minute, hitting rotation to self
- 2 vs 2 / 3 vs 3 half court, games
  - volley only to start
  - no jump (add in a bit later)
  - jump 2 step / 3 step attacks (working on 2 step for anyone I can work with)
  - 3m serve in

# Into our session
## Exercise 2 - Touch and go
6 vs 6, playing within 3m, run to end line and come back to play, must come back to a different position, after a 2 / 3 step attack (if you're attacking minded), defence getting low


Initiation will be a 3m / where you are working on float serve mechanics

## Before our next drill;
A little play around with how we fall / dive
- A key principle to me.. Be low first, it's a lot safer and a lot easier to dive when we're already low, going outo the outside of the leg you're falling on to take the weight

These are some technical techniques that are used, that we can go through

## Exercise 3 - 3 vs 3 half courts
- ball initiation is thrown at the net, we have to run, play low or dive to play it up, trying to get a touch and over (if we make an attack even better)
- no block on this one (till it's safer, we want everyone off the net within reason)
- IF the ball thrown at the net isn't played / dropped from the pass or doesn't go over, free ball over to start the rally (then restart)

We are playing the rest of the rally out as a normal game, attacking

## Exercise 4 - optional? Hitting lines (how I run with the 3 hitters etc)
- Just so we get some hitting and target practice in etc..
- We are going to be working on our 2 / 3 / 4 step approaches, working off of the setter
  - If you're feeling ambitious we can lower the set height to the top of the antenna (is the aim)

And lastly cannot decide between these 2..

## Exercise 5 - Wash drill
playing 6 vs 6, serve initiation
- winner has a ball thrown at the net, or thrown outside of what's comfortable, or a downball even (we can decide)

## Exercise 6  Endless drill
we play where the last mistake was with a mix of putting the ball at the net / into a position we must run for
