# Activity 1 -  3 vs 3
We're going to use a 3 vs 3 half court, 
- Initiation is a 3m serve (can work on float service mechanics, hand open, ball in front etc.)
- 2 front and 1 back, our front court is going to go to the middle and come out to defend the block
- Our attacks can be front court and a back court attack (or if you're feeling)

# Activity 2 - Slowing in game practice
A free ball in from the non attacking side, having 3 defenders and 3 blockers
(we'll alternate attacking and defending sides)

- Looking at how we form our block / bunch block and where we should be on court
- Free ball comes into the attacking side for a live pass
- Defending side looking at blocking footwork, what we read and how we defend (imagine 3 blockers and we're watching their eyes / feet)

Every 6 or so reps we'll swap sides on the attack

# Activity 3 - Cross court pepper using the middle and setter for the block
Putting activity 2 into a more live game, without position 1
- Serving from the endline (if it suffers we'll come in)

We can attack from 2 and 3 (even the pipe)

# Activity 4 - Game
If we read the block and bunch up right, you'll get 2 points, implementing the footwork that we had just used
