# Focus
Committing to our bunch block together, using footwork patterns, keeping on top of the attacker

# Warmup
- Blocking footwork, movements, (running on the spot with a partner and a ball)
  - Step out jump
  - Side shuffle jump
  - Shuffle jump
  - Crossover

# Activity 1 - 3 vs 3 half court
- 2 blockers 1 back court
 Blockers are going to start in the middle of the court and work their way out, using footwork patterns from the warmup
- Serve initiation from anywhere behind 3m line
- Add attacks, start with no jump, add jump and attacking in

# Activity 2 - Blocking eye work
Going to go through bunch blocking, where our blocking positions start

- 5 on blocking side, and someone to feed a ball in, 6 reps rotate round, 3 front 2 back
- 6 on other side and everyone can attack

On blocking side, 2 passers in court, someone to feed ball off court and thrown onto passer

# Activity 3 - Blocking from service (one ball kill style)
- 6 on serving side
- Variation of attacks (from first ball over, setter dump, middle, wings)

Can return 1 attack and reset

What are we looking at as blockers on the service line?
