# Consistent attacking approach

# resources
- 10+ players
- 10+ balls
- 1hour 30mins

# Focus
Having our attack approach used everytime, giving yourself a chance to swing, we're after using our 2 / 3 /4 step approach at least 80% of the time

# Warmup
Quick hand warmup with hand on head and then attacking the ball with your hand slightly spread and relaxed (using the head touch to show how to position your hand)

Adding in an attack approach with it

# Activity 1 - 2 vs 2 play
- Starting with a shuffle step into attack, into jumping

Looking at playing 2 vs 2 with the 3rd touch being an aggressive attack at the end (we want our setters to lead the attackers)

Advanced; start looking at playing with your wrist (wrist away or through), thinking about where your arm is finishing on that swing

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 3 play
- We are going to look at using our middle and pipe (so 1 front, 1 back and a setter)

Try to keep the tempo that we worked on the past couple of weeks and see how this plays out, using our step approaches and our hand control

# Activity 3 - cross court play
- We're going to have position 6 out of bounds, 2 players blocking (in serve receive 6 is in)

Playing positions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Look at how we add in the tempo attack again.


Lastly, playing to 25, you get a point for each attack, you lose a point if you don't attack or hit into 6

# Activity 4 - 6 vs 6 play
- If we run a tempo attack and score the point you win 4 points
