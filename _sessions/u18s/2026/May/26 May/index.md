# Attacking, slow to fast

# resources
- 7 / 8 players
- 10+ balls
- 1 coach
- 2 hours
- 2 courts

# Focus
Looking at creating slow to fast hitting approach rhythm, attempting to use this as an attack 80% of the time

# Warmup
We are going to play a 1 vs 1, 3 courts (1 court split into 3, 2 courts into 6 total)

- A serve in
- Pass to yourself, set to yourself and an attempt at attacking
- Must be a hit, jumping or not

# Activity 1 - Float service practice
- Going through a left step, toss, right, left, attack / float
- Just from the end line, experimenting with how we make a float attack happen

- Looking at experimenting with the slow to fast approach,
- The left step and toss can be slow going faster into the left, right (and vice versa for left handed players)

# Activity 2 - 3 vs 6
- A serve, into a free ball wash

- Looking at attacking from a back court, pipe attack
- Playing 15 points

The 6 side are playing a rotation and the 3 side are playing with a setter, defender and pipe attacker (also a defender)
- Serving on the 6 side with a free ball coming from the 6 side

- The 3 side have a chance to attack until 15 points, then rotate around

We are again looking at the approach being, slow to fast with our attack and arm swing also being slow to fast. Really exaggerating how slow to fast it is

# Activity 3 - 4 vs 5
- Creating a setter back court on the 5 side
- 3 front and 2 back, looking at creating attacking opportunities for the front court players

Still looking at attacking with an approach and slow to fast

You score a point for everytime you attack, not everytime you score, when there is an approach
