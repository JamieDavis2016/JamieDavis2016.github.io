# Pre-requisites
- 10+ players
- 10+balls
- 1 court

# Warmup (20 mins)
- Pepper
- Short ball, long ball, passing drill

- Hand Open contact
  - Over the net to a partner
- Under / over, (forearm passing) fed ball

# Half court drills
Setter going underneath the net each time 

Standing attacks
- 1 vs 1 with setter in the middle
- 2 vs 2 with setter in the middle
  - Deeper hits into back of court

Every 7 points, winners stay, loosers move to set
- Add in shuffle 
- Add in jump

# Technical hand open
Thumb up, thumb down
- Cross body, line
- kneeling and shuffling variations

# Setter at 1 back court
- 50% power and placement shots

# Game / scrimmage
First to 25
