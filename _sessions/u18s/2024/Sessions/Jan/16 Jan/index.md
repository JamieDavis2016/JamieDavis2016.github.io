# Focus
Block and Cover

## Warmup
- Pepper two different people
- Soft block, hard block 
- Footwork

When do we jump for our block?

## 1st Drill
- Partners, no net blocking
  - Hitting into the block
  - Core tight, shoulders locked

## 2nd Drill
- Drill in 3s
  - Hitting cross or line, team to shout where it's going

## 3rd Drill
- Meeting the block together 
  - Footwork

## 4th Drill
- 4 on one side, attacking point
- 6 Defensive
- Stop on the block, for defence
- Arms over for blocker
