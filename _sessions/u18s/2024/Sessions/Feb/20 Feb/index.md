# Warmup
- Footwork drill, 3 lines
- Pepper Variations
  - Ball up to self, side pass over
  - Ball up to self, volley, hit

## Squash with a hit
- In pairs, hit ball against the wall
  - First to 10 points

## Hitting progression
- On knee, use core, tighten stomach
- Shuffle under the net
- Shuffle over the net
1. Hand through down the middle
2. Cross body, thumb up, hit at 2
3. Wrist away, thumb down, hit at 1
